    Analysis

    Emmeline’s assessment

    Strongest Feature

    A low-rise, waterfront condominium right on Sentosa Cove with genuine marina views at many units — resort-calibre facilities (50m lap pool, clubhouse, gym) in a peaceful, yachting cocoon just off the mainland.

    Main Consideration

    Sentosa Cove is 99-year leasehold (the lease here dates from 2007, so a good decade-plus has already run), and priced at a luxury premium. The penthouse now coming to resale at ~$6.7m is a big-ticket, low-liquidity proposition — this is a lifestyle marquee, not a fast-return asset.

    Who This Suits

    Affluent buyers who want resort-style waterfront living on Sentosa Cove, with space, water views and strong privacy.

    Who Should Skip

    Yield-focused investors and anyone concerned about the running lease on a high-priced resale.

    Own-Stay Outlook

    Excellent for those who can absorb the premium — this is a genuinely serene, scenic place to live by the water.

    Investment Angle

    Scarce Sentosa Cove condo supply with hard-to-replace waterfront positioning supports a patient hold, but turnover is low and entry huge — prestige and lifestyle value more than near-term gains.
