New Launches Suitable for Families with Children

As of July 2026, families buying a new launch need to weigh school proximity, unit size, green space access, and resale liquidity. Here are the projects that score well across these family-critical factors.

By Emmeline Koh · Updated July 2026

01

Best Family-Friendly New Launches

Pinetree Hill

Why for families: Strong school belt — Dover Court International, ACS (International), SJII, Nan Hua Primary (1-2km). Ulu Pandan Park Connector and nearby green spaces. 3BR+ layouts with private lift options for multi-gen. Developer quality gives peace of mind for own-stay.

Consider: MRT distance may matter when children are older and need transport independence.

Dunearn House

Why for families: Turf City masterplan includes parks and community spaces. Bukit Timah school belt access. Well-proportioned family unit layouts. 360-unit development means intimate community feel. Surrounded by landed estate = safe, quiet environment.

Consider: Masterplan timeline — some amenities may come after you move in.

Amberwood at Holland

Why for families: D10 GCB enclave — prestigious address with strong school access (Nanyang Primary, CHIJ, Raffles Girls). Holland Village amenities. Larger units suitable for growing families. Freehold.

Consider: Premium pricing — entry threshold is higher than OCR alternatives.

Emerald of Katong

Why for families: D15 Katong is one of Singapore’s most family-friendly neighbourhoods. Established schools within reach (Tao Nan, CHIJ Katong, St Patrick’s). Vibrant food and lifestyle scene. Good resale track record for D15 new launches.

Consider: Katong area can be busy on weekends — factor in traffic patterns.
