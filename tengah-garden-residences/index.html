05 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

The first private condominium in Singapore’s Tengah “Forest Town” — a brand-new, smart-and-sustainable precinct with car-lite design, greenery at your door and direct service from Hong Kah MRT on the Jurong Region Line. Being first in a new master-planned town is genuinely exciting.

Main Consideration

The honest flip side is that Tengah is still being built out — the town, amenities, schools and the full retail scene aren’t fully matured yet, and won’t be for several years. It’s also far-gone in sales (low single-digit balance — a couple of 4-Bedder Premium units left with yard), so you’re buying the tail-end of a launch and waiting for the neighbourhood to catch up.

Who This Suits

Buyers who believe in the long-term vision of Singapore’s first smart town, want a new home in the west near the JRL, and are happy to grow with a brand-new heartland.

Who Should Skip

Anyone who needs fully-built-out amenities, schools and retail today, or who wants a broad choice of unit types — choice is extremely thin now.

Own-Stay Outlook

Good for patient, forward-looking families — a modern, green, smart home in a town designed around walking and greenery — provided you’re comfortable with the precinct still maturing.

Investment Angle

First-mover status in Tengah at ~$821 psf land cost leaves a credible growth runway as the town fills in and the JRL opens up connectivity, though the near term is a yield-and-amenity wait.

06 · Questions

FAQs

Is Tengah Garden Residences freehold?

No, it’s 99-year leasehold, lease commencing 2025.

Who’s the developer?

A joint venture of Hong Leong Holdings, GuocoLand and CSC Land Group.

How many units?

863 residential units, from 1 to 4 bedrooms plus penthouses.

When is TOP?

Expected 2029.

What district is it in?

D24 (Tengah, Western region).

What’s the pricing and availability?

From ~$2.706m for the 4-Bedder Premium; very limited stock left (a couple of 4-Bedder Premium units) as at August 2026.

How far is the MRT?

Directly served by Hong Kah MRT on the Jurong Region Line.

What’s nearby?

Tengah’s Forest Town heartland concepts, parks and future retail within the new precinct.
