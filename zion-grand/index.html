05 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

A big, full-range site (1 to 5-bedrooms plus penthouses) at the prime Kim Seng corner, co-developed by CDL and Mitsui Fudosan — serious build quality on a fresh July 2024 lease. It’s one of the more comprehensive new launches in that River Valley stretch.

Main Consideration

The TOP is far out — September 2032 is close to six years from now (as at August 2026), so this is a long wait for own-stay. The 4- and 5-bedroom flagships are heavily priced ($5M+), and you’re paying for the freshness of the lease with a leasehold tenure.

Who This Suits

Investors and patient buyers who are happy to wait out a ~2032 TOP for a prestige CDL-built address, and who want maximum layout choice in one project.

Who Should Skip

Anyone who needs to move in soon (TOP 2032 is a long way off), or who wants a compact 1-2 bedder — those tiers are already limited while the big suites dominate.

Own-Stay Outlook

Excellent for those who can wait — a full-amenity, large-site project from two blue-chip names in a superb location. The Havelock MRT walk and river-valley setting are real pluses; just be prepared for the long construction period.

Investment Angle

Blue-chip JV branding and a scarce large site in D3 give it strong rental and resale credentials long-term. The catch is capital locked up for ~6 years before TOP, so mind the holding cost.

06 · Questions

FAQs

Is Zion Grand freehold?

No, it’s a 99-year leasehold, from 15 July 2024.

Who is the developer?

CDL-MFA Altair Property Pte Ltd, a JV of City Developments Ltd (CDL) and Mitsui Fudosan.

How many units?

706 residential units.

When is TOP?

Expected 30 September 2032.

What district is it in?

District 3, at 3 & 5 Kim Seng Road.

Pricing and availability?

From about $1.45M for a 1-Bedroom + Study; smaller units limited, big premium suites available (as at August 2026).

How far is the MRT?

Close to Havelock MRT (TEL) and the River Valley area, with easy CBD/Orchard access.

Nearby amenities and schools?

Kim Seng’s riverfront dining, Robertson Quay and city malls nearby, plus city schools in the corridor.

Can I register interest?

Yes, Em can send the price list and showflat booking priority.
