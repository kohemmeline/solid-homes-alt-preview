    Analysis

    Emmeline’s assessment

    Strongest Feature

    A genuine seaside home in Sentosa Cove with curvaceous, ship’s-prow balconies where living rooms and balconies overlook the water — plus a sky pool, rainforest pool and clubhouse. Every penthouse even has a private pool.

    Main Consideration

    There is no MRT on Sentosa — the nearest is HarbourFront across the causeway, reached by car or the island’s shuttle (roughly a 10-minute drive), not a walk. This is a lifestyle location that leans heavily on driving and the resort atmosphere.

    Who This Suits

    Buyers who want a true waterfront/resort lifestyle in Singapore and are comfortable being car-dependent rather than MRT-reliant.

    Who Should Skip

    Anyone who needs rapid rail access to the CBD, or who’d find the exclusive, drive-everywhere Sentosa setup isolating.

    Own-Stay Outlook

    Idyllic for those who dream of waking up to water views in a curated, gated resort community — as long as you embrace the car-oriented, island lifestyle.

    Investment Angle

    Sentosa Cove’s waterfront stock is limited and the rental pool attracts a specific ultra-high-end tenant, but pricing is selective and liquidity can be thin at this level.

FAQs

Is Seascape freehold?

No, it’s a 99-year leasehold from 2007.

Who is the developer?

Seaview (Sentosa) Pte Ltd.

How many units are there?

151 units from 3-bedroom to penthouse.

When was it completed (TOP)?

2011, so a completed resale.

What district is it in?

District 4, at 57/59 Cove Way, Sentosa Cove.

What’s available and pricing?

3BR from ~$4.4M, 4BR from ~$5.6M, penthouses from ~$7.4M, with a range on the market (as at August 2026).

Which MRT is nearest?

HarbourFront MRT, across the causeway and not walkable — you’d drive or take the shuttle (~10 min).

What’s nearby?

Sentosa Cove’s marina, Quayside Isle dining, beaches and the resort precinct.
