    Analysis

    Emmeline’s assessment

    Strongest Feature

    An intimate enclave of just 20 bespoke strata-titled cluster villas right on Sentosa Cove — designed by Rene Tan’s RT+Q — with the boat-yachting lifestyle and marina setting that make it one of the most exclusive residential addresses in Singapore.

    Main Consideration

    Be clear this is 99-year leasehold (Sentosa Cove land is leasehold), and at ~$7m+ it’s a serious luxury outlay for a finished 2013 product. These are large villas in a niche, low-transaction enclave — so liquidity and price discovery are thin, and this is very much a lifestyle-first, not a flip-driven, buy.

    Who This Suits

    Ultra-high-net-worth buyers and family offices wanting a private, waterfront, low-rise compound on Sentosa Cove with the marina at the door.

    Who Should Skip

    Investors seeking liquidity, yield or quick value growth — the entry is huge, the market is thin and the return story is slower here.

    Own-Stay Outlook

    Spectacular for those who can afford it — private villas, endless water and greenery, car-lifestyle living at its most exclusive. A dream home before an investment.

    Investment Angle

    True Sentosa Cove scarcity and waterfront land value underpin a long-hold story, but expect low turnover and patient capital — this one’s about lifestyle and prestige, not near-term returns.

05 · Questions

FAQs

Is The Green Collection freehold?

No, it’s 99-year leasehold (Sentosa Cove land is leasehold from the state).

Who’s the developer?

Green Collection Pte Ltd, part of The Garcha Group / Elevation Developments.

How many homes?

A very private 20 cluster (strata) villas.

When was it completed?

TOP in 2013.

What district is it in?

D04 (Sentosa Cove / Harbourfront).

What’s the pricing and availability?

Resale villas from around $7.25m; the full batch of 20 was still being offered as at August 2026.

How’s the transport?

A car-driven lifestyle; access via Sentosa and the cove’s roads, not MRT-walkable.

What’s nearby?

Sentosa Cove marina, ONE°15, restaurants, and Resort World Sentosa nearby.

Want to register interest?

I can arrange a private viewing of whichever villa(s) you’re keen on and brief you on the strata details. —
