Frequently asked questions

How does the school catchment / balloting work?
Registration phases and distance categories determine priority. The catchment guide spells out the phases and risks.
Is it worth paying a premium to be within 1km?
Often yes for demand, but ballot risk means it’s not guaranteed. Balance school priority against neighbourhood quality and price.
Which districts suit families?
D10, D11, D5, D15 and D19 have strong school belts and family character — see the district pages for the projects.

The takeaway

Buy on the numbers and your real timeline — don’t let any single factor (school, district or launch hype) decide for you. If you’d like a second opinion on your specific situation, book a call.
