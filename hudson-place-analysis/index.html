03 · Analyst Take

What You Should Know Before Buying

Strengths

  • One-North is Singapore’s innovation hub with strong rental demand.
  • 400m to One-North MRT is highly walkable.
  • 327 units is boutique scale — quality over quantity.
  • Continued area development supports long-term appreciation.

Considerations

  • Smaller development means fewer facilities.
  • One-North commands premium psf pricing.
  • TOP 2028 means 2+ year wait.
  • Buyer pool is skewed towards professionals.
