Buying a Condo in District 6: City Hall / Clarke Quay / Fort Canning

Historic core with riverside living and city conveniences. Here’s the honest picture for buyers — both new launches and resale.

Quick Answer

District 6 suits professionals and investors wanting a central riverside address.. It’s a strong, defensible choice if you buy on location, school catchment and genuine lifestyle — be disciplined on price against the alternatives and don’t chase a hope of quick upside.

What I’d do

If you’re a family settling or an upgrader, District 6 is a legitimate choice — buy on location and school catchment rather than a hope of quick appreciation. If you’re investing, compare hard against what’s available one district over, and judge each project on its own numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use CPF to buy a condo in City Hall?
Yes — CPF OA can be used for private condos (subject to the applicable limits and lease-remainder rule). See the full CPF guide for the mechanics.
Is City Hall good as an investment right now?
It depends on your horizon and the specific project. Compare new launches against resale and the surrounding supply before committing — I cover this in the project pages.
New launch or resale in City Hall?
New launches in City Hall vary in availability and pricing; resale can offer better space-per-dollar. Decide on your timeline and how much renovation you want to take on.
