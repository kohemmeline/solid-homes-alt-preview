04 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

It’s the next phase of one-north at a price that undercuts most neighbours like Bloomsbury and ELTA on a psf basis. Elevated towers over 13m mean good views over low-rise Wessex Estate, and the whole Media Circle precinct is still building up — so there’s genuine growth story here.

Main Consideration

I’ll be straight with you — the MRT is not a doorstep walk. one-north MRT is roughly a kilometre away, so for most people it’s a short bus ride or cycle rather than a stroll. It’s a car-friendly/tech-park location more than a walk-to-MRT one, and TOP is still 2029, so it’s a wait.

Who This Suits

Tech-park workers, investors and families who value being inside one-north’s growth story and don’t mind trading off doorstep MRT for lower psf.

Who Should Skip

Anyone who needs a station within a comfortable walk, or who wants to move in soon — this one is built for the medium term.

Own-Stay Outlook

Good for those who work in one-north/Buona Vista and are happy to cycle or bus to the station — a well-designed, growing precinct — just factor in the rail walk and the wait.

Investment Angle

One-north is a landlord-friendly rental catchment with strong tenant demand, and the below-market entry psf gives upside once the precinct fills out — but the MRT gap is the thing to weigh against the newer stations.
