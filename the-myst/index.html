Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

It’s a thoughtfully-planned CDL condo right in the green Upper Bukit Timah corridor, and — honestly, a big plus — it’s genuinely close to rail: Cashew MRT is roughly a 6-minute walk on a sheltered footpath, with Bukit Panjang MRT also within reach. Green surrounds and workable connectivity in one package is a rare combination for this area.

Main Consideration

Only the larger 4 and 5-bedroom units remain, from roughly $3.23M up (as at August 2026) — so the appealing $998k entry price applies to smaller layouts that have long since gone. It’s a 99-year lease (from May 2023), and while TOP is pencilled for 2029 [verify: exact TOP], it’s still a launch with a wait.

Who This Suits

Families prioritising lush greenery, a trusted CDL build, and decent rail access at a walkable distance, who are comfortable with the premium price on the remaining big units.

Who Should Skip

Anyone hoping to grab the low entry price, or who wants a freehold/999 tenure or a fully finished project immediately.

Own-Stay Outlook

Lovely for nature-loving families — the Bukit Timah nature-reserve vibe and a real MRT walk make it a genuinely liveable, day-to-day pleasant spot.

Investment Angle

D23 land with this combination of greenery and rail is scarce, and CDL quality holds resale value well; the catch is the high entry price on remaining units now, so think medium-to-long term rather than quick flip.

Questions

FAQs

Is The Myst freehold?
No, it’s 99-year leasehold from 11 May 2023.
Who is the developer?
City Developments Limited (CDL) via CDL Aries Pte Ltd — one of Singapore’s most established developers.
How many units does it have?
408 residential units across two 24-storey blocks.
When is the TOP?
Expected in 2029 [verify: exact month], per the latest developer update.
Which district is it in?
D23, along Upper Bukit Timah Road in the Bukit Panjang/Dairy Farm area.
Is it sold out? What’s pricing?
Not fully, but only 4 and 5-bedroom units remain from about $3.23M upward (as at August 2026).
How close is it to the MRT?
Cashew MRT (Downtown Line) is about a 6-minute walk via sheltered footpath; Bukit Panjang MRT is nearby too.
What’s nearby / schools?
Close to Bukit Timah Nature Reserve, with Junction 10, Hillion Mall and Bukit Panjang shops around, and schools like Methodist Girls’ and Pei Hwa Presbyterian within reach.
