05 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

A 20-storey city-centre integrated project sitting right beside Maxwell, with Tanjong Pagar and the CBD on your doorstep — heritage hawker food (Maxwell Food Centre!) minutes away and genuinely walkable rapid rail. The “transformer” flexible layouts are a smart, modern touch.

Main Consideration

You’re paying premium D2 city prices for a 99-yr lease on a compact integrated block — no big-boy land/amenities, more of a pied-à-terre or metro-living proposition. Pricing of the 1-bedders sits well above $1.5M, and the studios carry the bulk of the remaining stock (as at August 2026).

Who This Suits

CBD professionals, investors and city-lovers who want a live-close-to-work address with easy MRT, restaurant scene and a lock-up-and-go type home.

Who Should Skip

Families wanting space, a kids’ clubhouse lifestyle or grounds to run around — this is an urban, compact-style project, not a suburban family estate.

Own-Stay Outlook

A joy for the city dweller — Tanjong Pagar’s dining, Maxwell hawker centre and the CBD all within a short stroll, with Maxwell and Tanjong Pagar MRT close by. Just know it’s compact and urban, not spacious and leafy.

Investment Angle

Prime Tanjong Pagar land with strong CBD rental demand makes it an attractive small-scale investment play, and the flexible layouts broaden the tenant pool. The 99-yr lease and fairly full pricing in such a competitive city-centre patch are the things to weigh.

06 · Questions

FAQs

Is TMW Maxwell freehold?

No, it’s a 99-year leasehold development.

Who is the developer?

Maxwell Residential Pte Ltd, a JV of the CEL Development / SingHaiyi / Chuan Investments group.

How many units?

324 residential plus 11 commercial units.

When is TOP?

Expected 30 June 2028 (the page data said Q1 2027 — [verify: exact TOP]).

What district is it in?

District 2, at 31 Tras Street in Tanjong Pagar.

Pricing and availability?

From about $1.32M for a Studio and ~$1.59M for a 1-Bed + Study; studios carry most stock (as at August 2026).

How far is the MRT?

Walkable to Maxwell and Tanjong Pagar MRT stations — one of the best-connected spots in the CBD.

Nearby amenities and schools?

Maxwell Food Centre, Tanjong Pagar dining and the CBD everywhere around, with city schools nearby.

Can I register interest?

Yes, Em can arrange a showflat visit and send the price list.
