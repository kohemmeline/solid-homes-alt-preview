05 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

A rare collection of just 39 ultra-exclusive homes sitting above the landmark Capitol Singapore complex — art, theatre, hotel and retail all under one roof, with the kind of heritage-city-centre setting you can’t build new today.

Main Consideration

It’s a tiny, highly exclusive project, so there’s very little on the resale market — just 2 units left (as at August 2026). Expect premium psf pricing that reflects the rarity, and understand that the facilities footprint is small because you’re part of a bigger integrated development.

Who This Suits

Collectors and connoisseurs who want a one-of-a-kind city-home tied to a heritage and arts landmark, not a cookie-cutter condo.

Who Should Skip

Anyone after resort-scale facilities, a big pool of neighbours, or who finds the ultra-rare, ultra-premium positioning a stretch.

Own-Stay Outlook

Supremely special for those who value heritage, culture and a true city-centre lifestyle — as long as you’re relaxed about the small, exclusive community and premium pricing.

Investment Angle

Scarcity is the whole game — 39 units in one of Singapore’s most storied locations means resale supply is permanently thin, which can support value, but liquidity is also limited.

06 · Questions

FAQs

Is Eden Residences Capitol freehold?

No, it’s a 99-year leasehold from 2011.

Who is the developer?

Capitol Residential Development Pte Ltd, part of the Capitol Singapore development.

How many units are there?

Just 39 exclusive units.

When was it completed (TOP)?

Around 2015/2016, so it’s a completed resale.

What district is it in?

District 6, at 11 Stamford Road.

What’s available and pricing?

2 Camellia units left (as at August 2026), from about $11.9M.

Which MRT is nearest?

City Hall MRT (EWL/NSL) is right beside the complex, with Bras Basah (CCL) also close.

What’s nearby?

Capitol Theatre, art galleries, the civic district, plus city-centre retail and dining.
