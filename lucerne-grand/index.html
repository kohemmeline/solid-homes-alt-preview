Why buyers are looking at it

The big draw is the station: you walk straight into Lakeside MRT on the East-West Line, two stops from Jurong East and its malls. Jurong Lake Gardens is a short stroll away, and Jurong Lake District, which the Government is building into the largest business district outside the city, is next door. Rulang Primary is among the schools nearby. The development has a lap pool, two clubhouses, a co-working lounge and a pets garden, and it carries BCA Green Mark Platinum Super Low Energy certification.

Analyst Take
Why Lucerne Grand Stands Out
Perspective from Emmeline Koh on the investment merits of Lucerne Grand.

Lucerne Grand is one of the most strategically positioned new launches in 2026. Here is why:

Jurong Lake District upside: Second CBD status is not aspirational — it is government-directed. The 120ha precinct builds on the existing Jurong East hub with billions in committed infrastructure. Early entrants benefit from long-term capital appreciation as the district matures.
True MRT doorstep: Few new launches offer the proximity Lucerne Grand has to an existing MRT station. Lakeside MRT on the East-West Line provides immediate connectivity without waiting for future lines — a rarity in the OCR.
CDL quality premium: CDL’s track record commands a premium in the resale market. Developments like The Tapestry, The Palette, and Whistler Grand have shown resilient pricing even in softer markets.
Natural asset: Jurong Lake Gardens is Singapore’s third national garden at 90 hectares — equivalent to 1.5 times the size of Gardens by the Bay. This green buffer is irreplaceable and adds significant lifestyle value.
Four top primary schools within 1KM: Rulang Primary, Jurong Primary, Nanyang Primary and Pryke Primary are all within the 1km zone – a rare school-belt concentration that is a decisive driver of family demand and resale premium in the western corridor.
Doorstep amenities: A first-storey retail, dining and supermarket podium right at your doorstep means everyday errands, groceries and F&B are on-site – the kind of doorstep convenience that shifts how buyers value a property and supports steady demand.

For buyers looking at the Jurong transformation story, Lucerne Grand offers the most direct exposure at the most accessible price point for entry to the second CBD narrative.
