Quick Answer

The Arcady at Boon Keng is a freehold development in Little India / Serangoon, District 8. Freehold in this area carries genuine scarcity value, so it suits buyers who plan to hold long-term and value land tenure over chasing the lowest entry price. The trade-off is that you’ll typically pay a premium over an equivalent 99-year launch nearby.

Proximity to good schools is one of the strongest price drivers in Little India / Serangoon. Check the actual 1km catchment for the primary school you have in mind — “within the district” is not the same as “within 1km” — and factor transport access to the nearest MRT and major roads into your choice of unit.

What I’d do

What I would do: work out your holding period first. If you are buying for the long term and value land tenure, the freehold here is defensible. If you are an investor chasing yield or a quick flip, compare hard against other freehold options in the same district. Don’t let the launch momentum or a single good-taste showflat do the deciding for you.

Frequently asked questions

Is The Arcady at Boon Keng a good investment?
It depends on your horizon and how you price the freehold tenure. Little India / Serangoon has genuine scarcity value you hold; it rewards patient buyers more than quick-flippers.
Is freehold here worth the premium?
In this area freehold is scarce, so the premium is more defensible than elsewhere — but it must be justified against a strong-lease 99-year alternative. Decide on your holding period.
New launch or resale near The Arcady at Boon Keng?
Compare like-for-like on per-square-foot price, remaining lease, and how much renovation you want. A resale unit can offer better space-per-dollar, but a new launch here may carry a fresher lease and lower immediate maintenance.
Can I use CPF or bank financing here?
Yes — CPF OA and bank loans both apply to private condos in Singapore, subject to the usual ABSD, LTV and lease-remainder rules. Confirm your financing approval before you commit so the tenure works for you.

The takeaway

The Arcady at Boon Keng is a solid option in Little India / Serangoon, District 8 — but only if it matches your timeline and tenure needs. Buy it for the location and the developer’s track record, not for a hope of quick appreciation.
