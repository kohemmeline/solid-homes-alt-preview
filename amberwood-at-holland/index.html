Launch recap · 25–26 September 2026

Amberwood at Holland: launch weekend results

  • Sim Lian sold 23 of 212 units (11%) over the launch weekend of 25–26 Sep 2026, at an average of about S$3,019 psf.
  • Of the 70 units released in phase one, about 33% were taken up.
  • 3-bedroom: S$2.516m–S$3.043m (avg S$2,962 psf). 4-bedroom: S$3.111m–S$4.024m (avg S$2,990 psf). 5-bedroom: S$3.912m–S$4.834m (avg S$3,026 psf).
  • Unit mix: 74 × 3BR (872–980 sq ft), 74 × 4BR (1,076–1,313 sq ft), 64 × 5BR (1,335–1,572 sq ft). There are no 1- or 2-bedroom units.
  • Remaining units are being released in phases.
