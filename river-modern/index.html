Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

This one sits right beside Great World MRT and just across from River Valley Primary School — genuinely strong CCR connectivity in a private, leafy pocket of D9. It’s a GuocoLand project and sold over 90% on launch day, which tells you how well it took.

Main Consideration

Be honest on price — this is a rich launch at around $2,800+ psf, one of the higher land-cost CCR sites. And as at August 2026 the inventory is very thin (only single-digit 2BR and 3BR left, with a handful of 4BR), so your stack choices are limited.

Who This Suits

Buyers who want a premium CCR address with an MRT next door, strong schools nearby, and don’t mind paying for the convenience and the GuocoLand finish.

Who Should Skip

The price-sensitive, or anyone who wants a wide range of units and views this late in the launch.

Own-Stay Outlook

Excellent for city dwellers who want everything walkable — MRT, shopping (Great World), the river and riverside parks. A well-put-together, convenience-first home if the numbers work.

Investment Angle

Great World MRT + D9 scarcity + strong launch performance is a solid story, but the high psf entry point means the return is really about the long game. Watch supply in the River Valley Green precinct.
