05 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

A rare big new launch in mature Toa Payoh — a 777-unit two-tower project in one of Singapore’s most established, dependable heartland towns with rail and schools built in around it. Getting a launch of this scale in Toa Payoh (and near Braddell MRT) is genuinely uncommon.

Main Consideration

Honest picture: availability is now mostly on the smaller, specific layouts — about 35 units left, dominated by 1-bedroom + study and 3-bedroom dual-key units (as at August 2026). If you wanted a mainstream mid-size family unit, choice is very tight. Give it a genuine look at what remains rather than expecting a full range.

Who This Suits

Compact/dual-key buyers and investors who love mature-town Toa Payoh convenience, proximity to Braddell/Braddell-road rail access [verify: exact MRT], and a trusted CDL-led build.

Who Should Skip

Anyone wanting a large freehold-style floorplan or a broad choice of layouts at this late stage of the launch.

Own-Stay Outlook

Good — Toa Payoh is a proven, self-sufficient heartland with everything nearby, and a rare big condo there is a comfortable long-term home if a remaining layout fits you.

Investment Angle

Mature-town scarcity plus a strong CDL/Frasers/Sekisui trio gives it solid resale and rental fundamentals; the dual-key units are an interesting rental play, though stock is now limited.

06 · Questions

FAQs

Is The Orie freehold?

No, it’s 99-year leasehold.

Who is the developer?

A joint venture of CDL, Frasers Property and Sekisui House.

How many units does it have?

777 units across two 40-storey towers at 10 & 12 Lorong 1 Toa Payoh.

When is the TOP?

Expected around 2028 (as at August 2026).

Which district is it in?

D12, Toa Payoh (Lorong 1).

Is it sold out? What’s pricing?

Mostly sold; about 35 units remain, mainly 1-bedroom + study and 3-bedroom dual-key layouts, around the $2.0k-2.5k psf range (as at August 2026).

How close is it to the MRT?

In the mature Toa Payoh heartland with rail within reasonable reach — Braddell MRT is the nearest widely-cited station [verify: exact walk distance to Braddell MRT].

What’s nearby / schools?

Toa Payoh’s established shops, malls and schools surround the project; it’s one of the more conveniently-located launches in a mature town.

How do I register interest?

Message via the contact page for the developer’s official remaining-unit list and pricing. —
