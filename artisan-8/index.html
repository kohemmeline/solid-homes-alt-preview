Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

A genuinely rare freehold in this part of town — an A&A of the former Sin Ming Centre into a petite 4-storey mixed development with just 34 homes above 8 shop units. For buyers who want a landed-style, low-rise, boutique feel on a permanent tenure, there’s very little like it in Sin Ming.

Main Consideration

Be honest about the transport — it’s not MRT-doorstep. Bright Hill MRT (TE7/Cross Island) is the nearest but it’s a proper walk, not a stroll; Upper Thomson MRT is also a stretch. Also, with only 34 units, it’s a small community (approx. a handful left of each type as at August 2026) — amenities and facilities are intimate rather than resort-scale.

Who This Suits

Buyers who value a freehold asset, low-rise exclusivity and the calm of the Sin Ming greens, and who don’t need rapid-rail access right at the door.

Who Should Skip

Anyone who needs a quick MRT walk, or who wants a full condo clubhouse, gym-and-lap-pool lifestyle with a big community behind it.

Own-Stay Outlook

Lovely for genuine home-lovers — a peaceful, green setting and a permanent freehold roof over your head. Just make sure the MRT walk and the small vibe work for your daily life.

Investment Angle

Freehold boutique stock in D20 is scarce, which gives an enduring-story appeal, but the thin unit count and the mixed-use (commercial shops below) mean liquidity and rental pool are limited — this is more a hold-and-enjoy proposition than a flip.
