01 · My Story

Why “Strategist” and Not “Agent”?

Because the two words describe fundamentally different jobs.

A property agent’s job is to close a deal. A property strategist’s job is to make sure you’re making the right decision — and sometimes, the right decision is to wait, or to buy something completely different from what you walked in wanting.

I’ve been in Singapore real estate since 2004. I’ve helped first-time buyers, investors managing multiple properties, families upgrading from HDB to private, and people trying to figure out whether their current property is working hard enough for them.

In that time, I’ve seen a lot of decisions go wrong — not because people were uninformed, but because they had the wrong kind of information. Marketing materials, biased comparisons, incomplete financial projections. The Singapore property market is complex enough without being misled by the people supposed to be helping you.

That’s why I built EmmelineKoh.sg as a knowledge platform first. Property guides, project analyses, market insights — available to everyone, whether you work with me or not. If you understand the landscape better, you make better decisions. And better decisions usually lead to a conversation with me anyway.

My Approach

Three Things I Always Do

Understand your full financial picture first

Before I recommend anything, I need to understand your income, CPF, existing loans, cash position and risk tolerance. Without this, any recommendation is guesswork.

Show you the data, not just the story

Every recommendation I make is backed by transaction data, pricing psf analysis, and comparable properties. You should be able to see exactly why I’m recommending something.

Give you the honest answer

Sometimes that means telling you not to buy yet. Or that a project you’re excited about has real risks. Honest advice, even when it’s not what you want to hear, is the only way I work.
