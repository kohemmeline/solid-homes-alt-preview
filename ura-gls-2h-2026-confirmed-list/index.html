Singapore GLS 2H 2026
Confirmed List Overview

The Urban Redevelopment Authority (URA) has released the Confirmed List for the second half of 2026, featuring 11 land parcels across Singapore that will yield approximately 6,870 residential units. This includes sites in prime districts (CCR), city fringe locations (RCR), and mass-market areas (OCR), as well as one Executive Condominium (EC) site.

Key Takeaway: This GLS release signals the government’s continued commitment to moderating property prices through steady land supply. Notably, the Jurong East white site and Town Hall Link site reinforce the Jurong Lake District’s transformation into Singapore’s second CBD. For buyers, the Berlayar Close and Marina Gardens Lane sites in the Greater Southern Waterfront area present compelling long-term growth potential.

FAQ

04

GLS Programme FAQs

What is the GLS Programme?

+

The Government Land Sales (GLS) Programme is Singapore’s system for releasing state land for private residential, commercial, and mixed-use development. The programme is divided into Confirmed List (sites that will be launched for tender) and Reserve List (sites that are triggered for tender only when a developer commits to a minimum bid price).

How many units will the 2H 2026 GLS add to the market?

+

The 2H 2026 Confirmed List will yield approximately 6,870 residential units across 11 sites, including Executive Condominium (EC) units. This steady supply helps ensure market stability and provides buyers with a range of options across different locations and price points.

What is the difference between RCR, CCR and OCR?

+

CCR (Core Central Region) covers prime districts 9, 10, 11, and the Downtown Core. RCR (Rest of Central Region) covers city fringe areas like Bukit Timah, Marine Parade, and Jurong East. OCR (Outside Central Region) covers mass-market areas like Hougang, Woodlands, and Tampines. Each category has different price points, buyer profiles, and investment characteristics.

When will these sites be available for purchase?

+

The Confirmed List sites will be launched for tender throughout the second half of 2026. Actual availability for home buyers depends on the tender process and development timeline. Typically, a site takes 6-12 months from tender award to public launch as a new condo project, with TOP (completion) usually 3-5 years after launch.

How does the GLS programme affect property prices?

+

The GLS programme is one of the government’s key tools for maintaining housing affordability. By releasing a steady supply of land, the government ensures that supply keeps pace with demand, which helps moderate price increases. A well-supplied GLS programme typically has a calming effect on property price expectations.

Which site has the most investment potential?

+

Based on location fundamentals and growth catalysts, the Berlayar Close site (Greater Southern Waterfront) and the Town Hall Link site (Jurong Lake District) offer the strongest long-term potential due to their positions within major transformation areas. The Orchard Boulevard site, while boutique-scale, benefits from prime location scarcity.
