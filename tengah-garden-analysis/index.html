03 · Analyst Take

What You Should Know Before Buying

Strengths

  • First-mover in Tengah’s Forest Town — capital appreciation potential.
  • Hong Leong + CSC + GuocoLand is a formidable consortium.
  • Singapore’s first smart, sustainable town.
  • Expected to be more accessible than RCR new launches.

Considerations

  • 2028/2029 TOP + developing town = 5-10 year horizon.
  • New town with no historical price data yet.
  • MRT timing depends on Jurong Region Line completion.
  • Resale liquidity may be thinner initially.
