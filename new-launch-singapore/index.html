02

Singapore New Launch Market — July 2026

Curated comparisons to help you shortlist based on your needs.

Best Options for HDB Upgraders

  • Lucerne Grand — Lakeside MRT doorstep, Jurong Lake District transformation, entry from ~$1.2M. Strong rental catchment.
  • Pinetree Hill — UOL quality near school belt. Entry ~$1.4M. Suited for upgraders with longer MRT walk tolerance.
  • Sora — Yuan Ching Road, Jurong. Competitive psf. Good for budget-conscious upgraders west side.
  • The Arcady — Boon Keng location. Accessible pricing, strong resale potential in mature estate.

Projects Near MRT

  • Lucerne Grand — Directly at Lakeside MRT (EW). Walk to station in under 3 minutes.
  • Skywaters Residences — Shenton Way MRT, future gateway of CBD. Ultra-convenient for professionals.
  • Newport Residences — Anson Road, walking distance to Tanjong Pagar MRT.
  • The Collective at One Sophia — Bras Basah MRT, city fringe. Ideal for young professionals.
  • One Marina Gardens — Marina South MRT (future station). Premium waterfront positioning.

Projects for Families

  • Pinetree Hill — Strong school belt (Dover Court, ACS International, SJII). Green spaces. 3BR+ options with private lift.
  • Dunearn House — Turf City masterplan. Bukit Timah school belt. Good family layout options.
  • Amberwood at Holland — D10 GCB enclave. Premium but strong family neighbourhood.
  • Emerald of Katong — D15 Katong. Family-friendly neighbourhood with established schools nearby.

More Affordable Entry Points

  • Sora — Entry pricing competitive for Jurong area. Potential upside from Jurong Lake District.
  • Seaside Residences — OCR East Coast. More accessible pricing than CCR/RCR comparables.
  • Meyer Blue — Freehold in D15 at competitive launch pricing for the tenure.
  • The Arcady — Boon Keng mature estate with strong rental yields potential.

Upcoming Launches Worth Waiting For

  • Town Hall Link — Jurong Lake District ~1,200 units. Mixed-use development adjacent to Jurong East transport hub.
  • Holland Plain — CCR Holland ~610 units. Prestigious location with limited upcoming supply.
  • Berlayar Close — Southern Waterfront ~695 units. Keppel Club redevelopment precinct.
  • Pine Grove (future GLS) — Adjacent to Pinetree Hill. Comparable positioning but pricing may adjust.
  • Tanjong Rhu Close — RCR Tanjong Rhu ~505 units. Waterfront living near city.
