Overview

About Marina Gardens Lane

Marina Gardens Lane is a residential site with commercial space on the first storey, released by URA on 13 Aug 2026 under the 2H 2026 Government Land Sales Confirmed List. It sits in Marina South (District 4, RCR), near the upcoming Marina South MRT station on the Thomson-East Coast Line and close to Gardens by the Bay and Marina Bay.

Status: GLS tender closed 15 Oct 2026, 12 noon; 1 bid received; award pending. No developer has been awarded the site, and no project name, launch date, facilities or prices have been announced.

Neighbour (as of Aug 2026): the adjoining earlier Marina Gardens Lane site was awarded in July 2023 at about S$1,402 psf ppr and is now One Marina Gardens, which had sold about 70.5% of its 937 units since its April 2025 launch.
