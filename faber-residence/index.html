Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

A genuinely rare low-rise, riverfront-style launch — set along the Pandan River and Ulu Pandan Park Connector in a quiet landed-enclave part of D5. It’s the kind of calm, low-density, nature-adjacent address that’s almost impossible to find in a brand-new condo.

Main Consideration

Be straight with yourself on the MRT — it is NOT a short doorstep walk. The nearest rail (Pandan Reservoir MRT) is about a 10-12 minute walk, and Clementi MRT is further, roughly four bus stops away [verify: which station is nearest/opened]. It’s 99-year leasehold, and only the bigger 4 and 5-bedroom units remain from about $2.8M up (as at August 2026).

Who This Suits

Low-rise nature lovers — families who put tranquillity, river views and the park-connector lifestyle above rail-at-the-door, and who can live with a short bus ride to the interchange.

Who Should Skip

Anyone who needs an MRT within a quick walk, or who wants a more urban, amenities-stuffed setting rather than a quiet low-rise one.

Own-Stay Outlook

Beautiful for those who value quiet low-rise living by the water — genuinely peaceful, well-built by strong developers; just be comfortable with the transport reality.

Investment Angle

Freehold-style scarcity in this low-density corner of D5 plus a strong developer trio (GuocoLand/Hong Leong/TID) gives it resale appeal; the trade-off is the less-than-doorstep MRT, so the play is long-term liveability rather than rail-driven flip.

Questions

FAQs

Is Faber Residence freehold?
No, it’s 99-year leasehold.
Who is the developer?
A joint venture of GuocoLand, Hong Leong Holdings and TID (Intrepid) — all well-established names.
How many units does it have?
399 units across low-rise blocks on the Faber Walk GLS site.
When is the TOP?
Expected around Q1 2029 (as at August 2026).
Which district is it in?
D5, off Faber Walk in the Clementi/West Coast area.
Is it sold out? What’s pricing?
Not fully, but mainly 4 and 5-bedroom units remain from about $2.8M up (as at August 2026).
How close is it to the MRT?
Not a doorstep walk; Pandan Reservoir MRT is roughly a 10-12 minute walk and Clementi MRT is further (about four bus stops) [verify: which station is nearest and when it opens].
What’s nearby / schools?
Beside the Pandan River and Ulu Pandan Park Connector, near Nan Hua and Clementi primaries, with Clementi/Jurong East shops a short ride away.
