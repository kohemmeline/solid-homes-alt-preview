05 · Questions

FAQs

Is Dorset Road Residences freehold?

No. It is a 99-year leasehold development.

Who is the developer?

A UOL-led consortium with Singapore Land Group and Kheng Leong, which won the Dorset Road GLS site for S$524.3 million.

How many units are there?

428 residential units across two 27-storey towers.

When is the launch / TOP?

Not launched yet. UOL targets a launch in the first half of 2027. TOP to be confirmed.

What district is it in?

The city-fringe (D8 / Farrer Park / Novena area), on Dorset Road.

What unit types are there?

A 2-to-4-bedroom mix (exact mix to be confirmed at launch). Indicative pricing around S$2,650-2,750 psf.

How close is the MRT?

About a 3-minute walk to Farrer Park MRT (North-East Line).

What schools are nearby?

SJI Junior and Hong Wen Primary are nearby, plus the Novena / D8 school belt.
