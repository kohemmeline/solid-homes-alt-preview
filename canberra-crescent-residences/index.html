04 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

This is a solid, family-friendly private condo in a still-maturing northern precinct, steps from the huge Bukit Canberra integrated hub and within walking distance of Canberra MRT. It’s also about as affordable as fresh private launches get in today’s market — the 3BR Premium under $2m is a rarity.

Main Consideration

Be honest on connectivity — Canberra MRT is walkable (about 750m, a good 8-10 minutes) but it’s not at your doorstep, and there’s a shuttle to help. The neighbourhood is still built-out, so amenity choices are thinner than in mature estates. And it competes directly with much cheaper ECs nearby, which you should weigh up if your budget is tight. Also note the TOP date — [verify: 2029 vs 2030] — so factor in a longer wait.

Who This Suits

Families and first-time private buyers who want space and a fresh 99-year lease at a softer price point, and who don’t mind being a bus/shuttle ride or a ~10-minute walk from the train.

Who Should Skip

Anyone who needs an MRT right at the gate, or who wants a mature, built-out neighbourhood with abundant F&B and retail right now.

Own-Stay Outlook

Good for those who value space, greenery and a quieter pace, with Bukit Canberra bringing a proper hub of amenities as the area matures. Just be comfortable with the suburban, car-dependent reality.

Investment Angle

You’re underwriting future demand here rather than proven rental yields — the North-South Corridor, RTS Link and Bukit Canberra development are the medium-term story to watch. It’s affordable now, which gives room, but note it sits alongside cheaper ECs.

05 · Questions

FAQs

Is Canberra Crescent Residences freehold?

No, it’s a 99-year leasehold running from 4 Nov 2024.

Who’s the developer?

Kheng Leong Co, together with Low Keng Huat (Singapore).

How many units are there?

376 residential units, mostly 3- and 4-bedroom layouts.

When is TOP?

Expected around 2029-2030 — worth double-checking the exact date given how recent the launch is.

Which district is it in?

D27, on Canberra Crescent in the Sembawang area.

What’s the availability and pricing?

3BR Premium from ~$1.99m, 4BR from ~$2.3m, with limited balance as at August 2026.

How far to the MRT?

Canberra MRT (NS12, North-South Line) is about 750m, an honest 8-10 minute walk, with a complimentary shuttle available.

Is this an Executive Condominium (EC)?

No — this is a private condo, no HDB eligibility or MOP restrictions.
