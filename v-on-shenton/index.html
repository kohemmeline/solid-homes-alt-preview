05 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

An iconic address right in the heart of the Marina Bay financial district — a 54-storey tower with the kind of skyline views you simply can’t replicate in a landed home. It’s mixed-use with the UIC office tower next door, so you’re living in the thick of Singapore’s “Wall Street”.

Main Consideration

Be honest on the MRT — the planned Shenton Way MRT station is still not open, so as at August 2026 the nearest operating stations are Tanjong Pagar and Downtown, which are a bit of a walk. It’s a resale now with only 2 penthouses left (as at August 2026), so choice is very limited and the entry price is eye-watering.

Who This Suits

High-net-worth professionals and CBD executives who want to live literally on top of the financial district and don’t rely heavily on the MRT.

Who Should Skip

Anyone who wants a genuine train at their doorstep, or who is shopping below the multi-million-dollar price point.

Own-Stay Outlook

Wonderful for those who love the energy, convenience and views of the city core — provided you’re comfortable with the current MRT access and can afford the scale of the units.

Investment Angle

Prime D01 land in the Marina Bay corridor is scarce and the Greater Southern Waterfront plan adds a long-term story, but at ~$1,938 psf you’re paying fully for that premium with resale-limited stock.

06 · Questions

FAQs

Is V On Shenton freehold?

No, it’s a 99-year leasehold from 29 November 2011.

Who is the developer?

UIC Investments (Properties) Pte Ltd.

How many units are there?

510 units across a 54-storey tower.

When was it completed (TOP)?

October 2017, so it’s a completed resale now.

What district is it in?

District 1, at 5 Shenton Way.

What’s available and pricing?

Only 2 four-bedroom penthouses left (as at August 2026), from about $16M.

Which MRT is nearest and how far?

Shenton Way MRT is planned but not yet open; nearest operating stations are Tanjong Pagar and Downtown, a moderate walk.

What schools/amenities are nearby?

Right in the CBD, so offices, hotels and dining abound; schools are a drive away.
