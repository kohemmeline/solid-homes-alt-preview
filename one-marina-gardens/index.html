04 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

It’s one of the first big launches into the emerging Marina South waterfront precinct — a brand-new district right beside Marina Bay’s financial core. For someone who wants to be at the heart of where Singapore is going next, there’s not much like it at this scale.

Main Consideration

Marina South is still being built out. The buzz and the skyline are there, but the day-to-day amenities aren’t fully in place yet — so this is a bet on the district maturing, not a move-in-tomorrow neighbourhood.

Who This Suits

Buyers who are comfortable being early to a precinct: city-minded owner-occupiers happy to trade immediate convenience for long-term upside, and investors with a longer horizon.

Who Should Skip

People who want everything walkable and settled today, or who need a quick TOP. New-estate living here means patience.

Own-Stay Outlook

Good for the long haul. If you love being beside the water and the city, it’s a rare kind of home — just understand the early stages of the precinct.

Investment Angle

Marina South is one of the most anticipated waterfront districts in Singapore. New marina-front supply is scarce and it sits right against the CBD, which gives it real appreciation potential as the area fills in.

05 · Questions

FAQs

Is One Marina Gardens freehold?

No, it’s a 99-year leasehold development.

Who is the developer?

Kingsford Development.

How many units are there?

937 residential units, as part of a mixed-use development.

When is TOP?

Expected around 2029.

What district is it in?

District 1, Marina South — the emerging waterfront precinct beside Marina Bay.

What’s available and pricing?

2-bedroom to 4-bedroom premium layouts, from about $1.88M (as at August 2026).

How close is the MRT?

Near Marina South MRT (Thomson–East Coast Line) and Marina Station as the network extends into the precinct.

What’s nearby?

Marina Bay Sands, the Marina Bay financial core, and the planned Marina South amenities as the district develops.
