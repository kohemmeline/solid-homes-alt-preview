05 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

Freehold luxury right at the Orchard Boulevard/premium Orchard fringe, built by a heavyweight JV (CDL + Hong Leong) and just a short stroll to Orchard Road and the Orchard Boulevard MRT. A rare freehold in this immediate luxury precinct.

Main Consideration

Pricing is seriously premium — a 2-bedroom + study at ~$4.9M (~$3,738 psf) is a big cheque for a mid-sized unit, and only 1 unit is left (as at August 2026). The hotel element in the mixed-use block isn’t everyone’s cup of tea.

Who This Suits

Investors and end-users who want a freehold trophy address in the Orchard belt and are comfortable paying top-of-market psf for scarcity.

Who Should Skip

Value seekers or anyone wanting a larger, family-scale home for the money — at this psf you’re buying location and freehold status more than space.

Own-Stay Outlook

Lovely for those who want to live in luxury beside Orchard Road with freehold permanence — everything is at your doorstep, if the pricing and small-remaining-stock work for you.

Investment Angle

Freehold within Orchard’s premium belt is genuinely scarce and holds scarcity value, but liquidity depends on finding a niche buyer willing to pay the premium.

06 · Questions

FAQs

Is Boulevard 88 freehold?

Yes, it’s freehold.

Who is the developer?

Granmil Holdings Pte Ltd, a joint venture of City Developments, Hong Leong Holdings and Lea Investment.

How many units are there?

154 units (plus a hotel block).

When was it completed (TOP)?

Around 2023, so it’s a recently completed resale/balance.

What district is it in?

District 10, at 86/88 Orchard Boulevard.

What’s available and pricing?

Only 1 two-bedroom + study unit left at about $4.9M (as at August 2026).

Which MRT is nearest?

Orchard Boulevard MRT (TEL) is close, with Orchard MRT also a short walk to Orchard Road.

What’s nearby?

Orchard Road’s full retail, dining and hotel scene is right at your doorstep.
