Buying Guides for First-Time Buyers in Singapore

Buying your first property is daunting — BTO, resale HDB or a condo? Start here.

Quick Answer

The right first step depends on your savings, income and timeline. If you can wait, BTO is the most affordable. If you want a home sooner or better resale value, resale HDB or a condo may suit you better.

Frequently asked questions

BTO, resale HDB or condo — which is best for me?

It depends on timeline and budget. BTO is most affordable but slowest; resale HDB balances cost and timing; a condo suits larger budgets and future-proofing. See the first-timer guide.

How much do I need to buy my first property?

It depends on purchase price, LTV, ABSD and CPF. The how-much and salary guides break down the real cash + CPF figures.

Can I use CPF for my first home?

Yes — CPF OA funds can be used for your first property, subject to limits. The CPF guide explains the mechanics.

The takeaway

Buy on the numbers and your real timeline — don’t let any single factor (school, district or launch hype) decide for you. If you’d like a second opinion on your specific situation, book a call.
