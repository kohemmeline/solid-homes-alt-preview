About Holland Plain

Set in the coveted Holland Plain enclave of District 10, this CCR development offers a rare blend of cosmopolitan village charm and serene residential exclusivity. Steps from the vibrant Holland Village dining and entertainment scene, and surrounded by premier schools and lush greenery, Holland Plain defines the pinnacle of urban-suburban living.

As a URA GLS 2H 2026 Confirmed List site, this development represents one of the most anticipated residential launches in Singapore. Register your interest with Emmeline Koh for priority queue numbers and exclusive preview access. Official 2H2026 GLS details: this CCR site on Holland Plain has a plot ratio of 1.8 with an estimated yield of about 610 residential units – the third and largest site released via GLS in the Holland Plain precinct (the most recent Holland Plain site was awarded May 2026). It sits in an exclusive private landed enclave near the Brizay Park GCB area and within 1km of Methodist Girls’ School, though the two closest MRT stations (King Albert Park and Sixth Avenue) are more than 1km away.
