    Analysis

    Emmeline’s assessment

    Strongest Feature

    A waterfront collection by Ho Bee with just 91 spacious luxury units, generous 3- and 4-bedroom layouts through to sky villas, a pool with spa pools, clubhouse and 21 private berths — pure resort living on the Cove.

    Main Consideration

    Only 1 penthouse is left (as at August 2026), so choice is extremely thin. And like all of Sentosa, there’s no MRT — you’re car/shuttle-dependent to HarbourFront, so this suits a specific lifestyle.

    Who This Suits

    Buyers drawn to an exclusive waterfront community with private berths and a resort feel who don’t need daily rail commuting.

    Who Should Skip

    Anyone wanting an MRT nearby or a range of units to compare — you’re down to essentially one penthouse.

    Own-Stay Outlook

    A serene, high-end waterfront home for those who truly value the island resort lifestyle and the privacy of a small, curated community.

    Investment Angle

    Scarcity of waterfront freehold-style stock in Sentosa Cove supports value, but high entry psf and a thin, exclusive buyer pool mean careful entry.

05 · Questions

FAQs

Is Turquoise freehold?

No, it’s a 99-year leasehold.

Who is the developer?

Ho Bee Cove Pte Ltd (Ho Bee Land).

How many units are there?

91 units including 3/4-bedroom, penthouses and sky villas.

When was it completed (TOP)?

2010, so a completed resale.

What district is it in?

District 4, at 51/55 Cove Drive, Sentosa Cove.

What’s available and pricing?

1 penthouse left at about $4.4M (as at August 2026).

Which MRT is nearest?

HarbourFront MRT, not walkable from Sentosa — you’d drive or take the shuttle.

What’s nearby?

The Cove’s marina, Quayside Isle, beaches and resort dining.
