About the Orchard Boulevard site

Orchard Boulevard is a residential Government Land Sales (GLS) site in District 10, in the Core Central Region. URA is offering it on a 99-year lease, with a site area of about 3,438 sqm, a maximum gross floor area of about 9,627 sqm and an estimated 110 homes.

The tender closes on 29 October 2026 at 12 noon. Until URA announces the bids and awards the site, there is no developer, project name, unit mix, facilities list, price or launch date. I’ll update this page as each of these is officially announced.
