First Timer Buying Guide: BTO, Resale HDB Or Condo?

By Emmeline Koh · Updated 2026-06-18

As a first-time buyer in Singapore, you have three main options: BTO HDB, resale HDB, or private condo. Each path has different financial requirements, timelines, and lifestyle outcomes. Here is a clear framework for choosing the right one.

Buying Guide
6 min read

Quick Answer

Your first property decision comes down to budget and timeline: BTO is cheapest but takes 4–5 years, resale HDB is faster with grants available, and a private condo offers the most flexibility at a higher entry price.

Key stat: BTO from ~$300K, resale HDB from ~$500K, condo from ~$1.2M
Updated: July 2026
By: Emmeline Koh, CEA Reg R045542G

01

Option 1: BTO HDB

Best for: Young couples with time on their side and limited savings.

• Waiting time: 3-5 years from application to key collection

• Price: Highly subsidised, typically S$300K-S$500K for 4-room

• Grants: Up to S$80,000 EHG for first-timer families

• Down payment: 10% (or 20% for shorter leases)

• Monthly mortgage: S$800-S$1,500 (CPF covers most or all)

BTO is the most affordable entry point but requires patience. The waiting time can be a dealbreaker if you need a home sooner.

02

Option 2: Resale HDB

Best for: Buyers who need a home immediately and want location flexibility.

• Move-in: Immediately after completing the purchase (2-4 months)

• Price: S$400K-S$700K for 4-room in mature estates

• Grants: Up to S$80,000 EHG + S$20,000 proximity grant

• Down payment: 25% (10% for HDB loan, 25% for bank loan)

• Monthly mortgage: S$1,200-S$2,000

Resale HDB offers immediate housing and better location options than BTO, at a higher price.

03

Option 3: Private Condo

Best for: Buyers with higher income, CPF savings, and the budget for better lifestyle.

• Price: S$1.2M+ (2BR in OCR)

• Down payment: 25% minimum (at least 5% cash)

• Monthly mortgage: S$4,000-S$6,000+

• No grants available

• Facilities and lifestyle benefits

04

How to Decide

Ask yourself three questions: How quickly do you need a home? How much cash and CPF do you have? What are your plans for the next 5-10 years?

If you want the upgrade path: start with resale HDB in a good location, build equity for 5-7 years, then upgrade to a condo. This is the most common and proven path for Singapore families.

05

Bottom Line

There is no universal right answer. The best choice depends on your personal circumstances. If you want personalised advice, book a first-time buyer consultation.
