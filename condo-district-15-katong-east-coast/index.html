Buying a Condo in District 15: Katong, Meyer & East Coast

One of Singapore’s most liveable coastal districts — mature neighbourhood, strong schools and genuine character. Here’s the honest picture for buyers, for both new launches and resale.

Updated 2026-10-22

Quick Answer

District 15 is a strong, family-friendly pick if you value school catchment and a mature coastal neighbourhood over pure capital upside. Supply of new launches is thin and firm on price — go in knowing you’re paying for lifestyle, and be disciplined against the RCR alternatives one district inland.

02

Why buyers choose District 15

D15 runs from Mountbatten through Katong to the East Coast. It’s a mature, established belt with heritage shophouses, a famous food scene and real walkability — qualities that hold up well over time. For families, the school catchment is a genuine draw.

03

Schools & transport

Proximity to primary schools within 1km is a key driver for D15 buyers. On transport, the area sits within reach of the East Coast Line and major road corridors (ECP / MCE) — handy if you drive, and reasonable for MRT if you pick the right project. Check each project’s specific walkability before committing.

04

What I’d do

If you’re a family upgrading or settling, D15 is a legitimate choice — buy on location and school catchment, not on a hope of quick appreciation. If you’re an investor chasing yield or short-term flip, compare hard against what one district inland offers; the lifestyle premium here doesn’t always translate to stronger returns.

05

Frequently asked questions

Can I use CPF to buy a condo in District 15?

Yes — CPF OA can be used for private condos (subject to the applicable limits and lease-remainder rule). See the full CPF guide for the mechanics.

Is freehold worth the premium in Katong?

For a home you’ll hold long-term, freehold in Katong carries peace of mind, but the premium must be justified vs leasehold with strong remaining lease. It depends on your horizon.

New launch or resale in D15?

New launches in D15 are limited and priced firmly; resale can offer better space-per-dollar. Decide on timeline and how much renovation you want to take on.

The takeaway

District 15 rewards you with lifestyle, schools and a genuine neighbourhood — pay for those, not for a hope of quick upside. Be disciplined on price against the RCR alternatives.
