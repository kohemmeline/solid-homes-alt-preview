05 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

Singapore’s first W-branded home-hotel residence — a W Hotel on your doorstep plus 683 luxury homes right in the Marina Bay are (a W Residences above the W Singapore hotel). Global brand cachet, high-spec finishes and arguably the most exciting hotel-integrated living product on the market right now.

Main Consideration

This is priced at a serious luxury premium, and it’s 99-year leasehold — roughly seven of those years have already ticked by since the lease started in Dec 2021. If long-term-value-per-dollar is everything to you, the W premium and the shorter remaining lease term are honest considerations. Plenty of units still available (as at August 2026), so there’s choice — but that also tells you the market is digesting the pricing.

Who This Suits

Wealthy owner-occupiers and brand-lovers who want a five-star lifestyle, hotel services and a world-class location, and who accept the leasehold and the premium.

Who Should Skip

Value-focused investors chasing yield — sub-2% rentals don’t excite here — and anyone uncomfortable with a 99-year term that’s already a few years in.

Own-Stay Outlook

Exceptional if you want the full resort-and-city lifestyle — think W pool, spa, F&B and concierge at home in Marina Bay. It’s a spectacular way to live, if you can live with the premium.

Investment Angle

An ambitious, image-driven product in a prime location with mature central-area rental demand, but the yield story is modest and the leasehold term shortens over time — treat it as a prestige hold, not a yield play.

06 · Questions

FAQs

Is W Residences freehold?

No, it’s a 99-year leasehold commencing 27 December 2021.

Who’s the developer?

Boulevard Development Pte Ltd, a subsidiary of IOI Properties Group (Malaysia).

How many units?

683 residential units.

When is TOP?

Expected around 2028/2029 (reports vary between Q1 2029 and 2H 2028).

What district is it in?

D01 (Marina Bay / Boat Quay / Raffles Place).

What’s the pricing and availability?

From about $1.778m for a 1-Bedder; a wide selection across all layouts was still available as at August 2026.

How’s the transport?

Central Marina Bay — minutes to Raffles Place/Downtown, and downtown MRT lines nearby.

What’s nearby?

Marina One, MBFC, the waterfront, Gardens and the CBD — arguably the best-connected central location.
