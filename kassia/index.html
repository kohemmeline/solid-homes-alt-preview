04 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

It’s freehold — which is genuinely rare for a new launch — and it’s the final residential plot in the well-liked Flora Drive enclave. Between the tenure, the greenery and the Changi coastal-east lifestyle, it stands out from the usual 99-year launches.

Main Consideration

Freehold and location come at a price, and TOP is around 2029. The east also has plenty of other new supply to compare, so it pays to weigh options before committing.

Who This Suits

East-side upgraders and families who want a quieter, greener pocket of the east with good schools nearby — and who value holding onto freehold tenure.

Who Should Skip

Buyers who need early completion, or who’d rather be nearer the more established Tampines/Bedok centres.

Own-Stay Outlook

Good for own-stay — peaceful, green, with Changi airport, the coast and a cluster of schools close by, and freehold to hand down.

Investment Angle

Freehold scarcity plus Changi Business Park and the airport employment base give it longer-term holding appeal — though as with any freehold, you pay a premium up front that rewards time.
