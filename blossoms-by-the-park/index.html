05 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

It’s the plum location at one-north — a 99-year leasehold mixed-use tower planted right between two MRT stations, with Buona Vista MRT only about a 250–300m stroll away and Rochester Mall and Star Vista on the doorstep. For a brand-new launch in D05, the connectivity is genuinely excellent.

Main Consideration

The residential units are fully sold out (as at August 2026), so this is no longer a residential opportunity — only the commercial restaurant unit is left, at about $13M. If you’re after a home here, you’re out of luck; if you want the food-and-beverage/lifestyle play, it’s a very different proposition.

Who This Suits

Commercial investors looking at the food-and-beverage space in a high-traffic one-north location, right next to the MRT.

Who Should Skip

Anyone hoping to buy a residential unit — they’re all gone (as at August 2026). Also skip if the restaurant/lifestyle commercial space isn’t your lane.

Own-Stay Outlook

Not really applicable now for homes since the residential stock has sold out — this is a commercial play at this stage of the launch.

Investment Angle

One-north is a proven research-and-tech hub with strong rental demand and the “MRT effect” in play, so the commercial lot taps a busy footfall corridor — but it’s a substantial single-ticket investment at ~$13M.

06 · Questions

FAQs

Is Blossoms By The Park freehold?

No, it’s a 99-year leasehold.

Who is the developer?

EL Development (Buona Vista) Pte Ltd.

How many units are there?

275 residential units plus 1 commercial lot.

When is TOP?

Expected around 30 June 2028; it’s still under construction.

What district is it in?

District 5, at 9 Slim Barracks Rise, one-north.

What’s available and pricing?

Residential is sold out; only the ~5,147 sqft Restaurant commercial unit is left at about $13M (as at August 2026).

Which MRT is nearest?

Buona Vista MRT is about 250–300m away (3–5 min walk), with one-north station also close.

What’s nearby?

Rochester Mall, Star Vista, one-north’s tech hub, and a strong cluster of schools including ACS (Independent).
