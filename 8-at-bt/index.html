No. 8@BT is a 99-year leasehold development.

It is developed by Bukit Sembawang Estates.

How many units does 8@BT have?

158 units across two 20-storey blocks, 1BR to 4BR plus Penthouses.

When is the expected TOP?

Q4 2027.

District 21 (Upper Bukit Timah / Clementi Park / Ulu Pandan), at Bukit Timah Link.

Yes — about a 2-minute walk from Beauty World MRT (Downtown Line).
