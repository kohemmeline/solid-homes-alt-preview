05 · Questions

FAQs

Is ARC 380 freehold?

Yes. ARC 380 is a freehold commercial development.

Who is the developer of ARC 380?

It is developed by Tong Eng Brothers, with architecture by DP Architects.

What type of property is ARC 380?

A mixed-use commercial development with strata office units and retail/F&B outlets.

When was ARC 380 completed?

It obtained TOP in 2018 and is fully completed.

What district is ARC 380 in?

District 8 (Little India / Farrer Park), at Jalan Besar / Lavender Street.

Is ARC 380 near MRT?

Yes — Bendemeer MRT (~300m), Boon Keng (~690m) and Lavender (~700m).

What facilities does ARC 380 have?

A 25m pool, gymnasium, rooftop function room, BBQ area and landscape deck.
