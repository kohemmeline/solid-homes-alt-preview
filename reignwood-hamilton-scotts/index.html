05 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

Freehold land in the heart of D09 Newton, and it’s famously the only residence in Singapore with sky garages — big, airy, ultra-luxury homes with a serious wow factor and a genuinely short walk to Newton MRT.

Main Consideration

It’s ultra-luxury pricing at ~$10.2M for a 3-bedroom, so this is a narrow market. With only 2 units left (as at August 2026), there’s very little to choose from, and the sky-garage lifestyle isn’t for everyone.

Who This Suits

Affluent families who want a freehold trophy home in D09 with rare features, great schools nearby and MRT convenience.

Who Should Skip

Any budget-conscious buyer, or anyone who doesn’t need (or want) the sky-garage, super-large-scale statement this project makes.

Own-Stay Outlook

Excellent for those who value space, privacy and prestige — freehold, well-located in Newton, and within walking distance of excellent schools and Newton Food Centre.

Investment Angle

Freehold scarcity in D09 plus a short MRT walk is a strong long-term story, though the high entry price means the buyer pool for resale is small and niche.
