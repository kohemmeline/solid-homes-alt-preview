04 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

Part of the brand-new Lentor township, and it’s one of the more accessible — Lentor MRT (TE5) is about a 5-minute walk, with Lentor Hillock Park basically at your door and good primary-school options around. Walkable rail plus green surrounds in a freshly-planned estate is a strong, honest combination.

Main Consideration

It’s been on sale a while and roughly half is gone — the units left now skew to 2-bedroom (many available), with a handful of 3 and 4-bedroom premium units remaining (as at August 2026). It’s 99-year leasehold, and the Lentor Hills estate itself is still filling in, so the neighbourhood is not yet mature.

Who This Suits

First-timers, upgraders and families drawn to a new green township, who value a real MRT walk and value-for-money compared to neighbouring Lentor launches.

Who Should Skip

Anyone who wants a fully mature, built-out neighbourhood immediately, a freehold/999 tenure, or who needs a wide range of big 4/5-bedroom choices (those are now scarce here).

Own-Stay Outlook

Promising — the walkable TEL rail, the park at the door and the family-friendly planned estate make it a solid long-term home as the township matures.

Investment Angle

Lentor is one of the more active new-township plays, and being one of the closer projects to Lentor MRT with Singapore/Orchard via TEL connectivity gives it real rental and resale appeal; just buy with a medium-term view as the estate completes.

05 · Questions

FAQs

Is Lentor Gardens Residences freehold?

No, it’s 99-year leasehold.

Who is the developer?

Kingsford Development (Kingsford Lentor Project Pte Ltd).

How many units does it have?

About 502 units across low/mid-rise blocks in the new Lentor Hills estate.

When is the TOP?

Expected around Q1 2029 [verify: Q1 vs Q4 2029], per the latest update.

Which district is it in?

D26, Lentor (Mandai/Upper Thomson area).

Is it sold out? What’s pricing?

Roughly half sold; 2-bedroom units are the most available from about $1.56M, with limited 3/4-bedroom premium left (as at August 2026).

How close is it to the MRT?

About a 5-minute walk to Lentor MRT (TE5, Thomson-East Coast Line) — walkable and genuinely useful.

What’s nearby / schools?

Lentor Hillock Park at the door, Thomson Nature Park nearby, and a strong school belt (Anderson Primary, CHIJ St. Nicholas, etc.) in the wider area.
