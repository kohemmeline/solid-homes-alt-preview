03 · Analyst Take

What You Should Know Before Buying

Strengths

  • Freehold tenure in D15 is rare for a development of this scale.
  • Katong location is one of Singapore’s most beloved residential areas.
  • 816 units is substantial enough for good facilities.
  • Dual MRT access provides strong connectivity upside.

Considerations

  • Freehold commands a significant premium.
  • TOP 2028 means a longer wait.
  • Katong can have heavy traffic during peak hours.
  • Entry price for freehold in D15 is premium.
