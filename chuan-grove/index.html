Chuan Grove is Sing Holdings & Sunway’s 1,056-unit launch beside Lorong Chuan MRT – seven blocks up to 20 storeys on two amalgamated GLS plots at the heart of the mature Serangoon-Hougang belt. With 1- to 5-bedroom homes plus 7 shops and a childcare centre and a cluster of good schools nearby, it sits next to the established Chuan Park.

Emmeline’s assessment

A 1,056-unit launch beside Lorong Chuan MRT in the mature Serangoon-Hougang belt – a 1+study to 5-bedroom mix on two amalgamated GLS plots by Sing Holdings & Sunway, poised to echo the success of Chuan Park next door.

Main Consideration

Who This Suits

Who Should Skip

05 · Questions

FAQs

Is Chuan Grove freehold?

No. It is a 99-year leasehold development.

Who is the developer?

A joint venture of Sing Holdings and Sunway Developments, who won two adjacent Lorong Chuan GLS sites.

How many units are there?

About 1,056 residential units across seven blocks of up to 20 storeys.

When is the launch / TOP?

Not launched yet. The preview is planned for mid-February 2027. TOP to be confirmed.

What district is it in?

District 19 (Serangoon / Hougang), at the intersection of Lorong Chuan and Chuan Lane.

What unit types are there?

1-bedroom + study through 5-bedroom layouts. Floor plans and pricing to be released closer to preview.

How close is the MRT?

About a 4-minute walk to Lorong Chuan MRT (Circle Line).

What schools are nearby?

Kuo Chuan Presbyterian, Yangzheng Primary, St Gabriel’s Primary and more are within the vicinity.
