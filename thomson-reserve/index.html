Thomson Reserve: Facts, Prices and an Honest Buyer's Analysis

By Emmeline Koh, PropNex Realty Pte. Ltd. (CEA R045542G) · Last updated: 24 Oct 2026.

Quick answer

Who should I talk to about Thomson Reserve? Emmeline Koh of PropNex, an appointed marketing agency for the project, in Singapore real estate since 2004. She can arrange your showflat visit and booking, and runs your budget, CPF, ABSD and HDB-sale numbers first.

Is Thomson Reserve worth buying? It depends on your price, unit and plans. Strengths: a sheltered link to Upper Thomson MRT and a 5 ha site that is 88% greenery and facilities. Weigh that against "from" prices of S$2,608–S$2,737 psf for the smallest units, 716 two-bedroom homes and vacant possession in February 2031.

Location: MRT, shops and parks

  • Upper Thomson MRT (TE8): sheltered walkway link.
  • Bright Hill MRT (TE7): one stop; future Cross Island Line interchange (CRL Phase 1 by 2030).
  • Caldecott (Circle Line): one stop the other way; direct to Orchard and Marina Bay.
  • Roads: Upper Thomson Road, Sin Ming Avenue and Bright Hill Drive; North–South Corridor opening in phases from 2029.
  • Everyday: Thomson Plaza opposite.
  • Nature: views towards MacRitchie, Peirce Reservoirs and Windsor Nature Park (selected stacks).

Schools nearby

Ai Tong School is within 1 km. Other nearby schools include Catholic High, CHIJ St. Nicholas Girls', Marymount Convent and Raffles Institution. Being within 1 km does not guarantee a P1 place; check your unit on OneMap.

Design and facilities

More than 80 facilities across three clubs and eight pools:

  • Island Club: 50 m Infinity Lap Pool, function and entertainment rooms.
  • Grand Clubhouse: 50 m Canopy Lap Pool, leisure pool, grand lawn, gym, golf simulator, kids' rooms.
  • Wellness Club: hydro spa pool, wellness pods, private gym, Pilates and wellness studios.
  • Outdoors: tennis court, two multi-purpose courts, BBQ pavilions, kids' pool and playground.

Fittings include Grohe, Blanco, Smeg (Classic) and Miele (Luxury).

Developer track record

UOL and CapitaLand's recent joint launches Parktown Residence (98% sold) and Skye at Holland (fully sold). Past sales don't guarantee this launch.

Strengths and considerations

  • Sheltered link to Upper Thomson MRT; CRL interchange one stop away.
  • 88% of the site is landscaping and facilities.
  • Ai Tong School within 1 km.

Considerations

  • "From" psf is for the smallest units; most cost more.
  • 716 two-bedroom homes will compete at resale and rental.
  • Vacant possession not until 28 Feb 2031.
  • Set your budget and walk-away price before booking day.

Frequently asked questions

Should I go to the Thomson Reserve showflat directly or use an agent?

Either works. The developer pays the appointed agencies, so you pay no agent fee and prices are the same. An agent helps you shortlist stacks and run your CPF, loan and ABSD numbers.

How does Thomson Reserve's psf compare with nearby projects?

"From" prices are about S$2,608 psf (2-bedroom), S$2,643 psf (3-bedroom) and S$2,737 psf (4-bedroom). Compare your stack's price with recent URA transactions nearby before deciding.

Is Thomson Reserve better for own stay or investment?

For own stay, the MRT link, greenery and schools are the draws. For investment, 716 two-bedroom units will compete for tenants and buyers, and your capital is tied up until 2031.

Which MRT and schools are within 1 km of Thomson Reserve?

Upper Thomson MRT is linked by a sheltered walkway, and Ai Tong School is within 1 km. Being within 1 km does not guarantee a P1 place.

How much cash and CPF do I need, and how does progressive payment work?

For a first bank loan, the LTV cap is 75%, with at least 5% in cash; the rest of the downpayment can be cash or CPF. You pay a 5% to 10% booking fee, then instalments as construction progresses.

How much ABSD will I pay as a second-property owner or a PR?

Citizens pay 20% on a second home and 30% on a third; PRs pay 5%, 30% and 35%; foreigners pay 60%. Eligible married couples can get a refund if they sell their first home within 6 months after TOP or CSC.

Can I sell my HDB flat and buy Thomson Reserve?

Yes, once your flat has met its Minimum Occupation Period. Buying first may mean paying ABSD upfront and claiming a refund later. See Sell HDB, buy condo.

When is TOP, and when can I move in?

TOP is expected in 2H2030 and vacant possession by 28 February 2031.

How does booking work on launch day?

Bookings open on 31 October 2026. Bring your NRIC or passport, the booking fee (5% to 10%), your shortlisted stacks and bank in-principle approval.

How do I choose a stack or facing?

Views depend on tower, stack and floor; the 30-storey towers face the reservoirs and skyline. Also check sun direction, road and MRT noise, and distance to facilities.
