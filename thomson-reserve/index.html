Project overview

Upper Thomson living, beside the MRT

Thomson Reserve is a 1,268-unit, 99-year leasehold condominium on Bright Hill Drive in District 20, where the former Thomson View stood. It is jointly developed by UOL Group, Singapore Land Group (SingLand) and CapitaLand Development.

The site is about 5 hectares. It holds six towers: four of 21 storeys (the Classic Collection) and two of 30 storeys (the Luxury Collection), with 2- to 5-bedroom homes. The developer expects TOP in 2H2030 and vacant possession by 28 February 2031.

Why buyers are looking at Thomson Reserve

  • About a 2-minute walk to Upper Thomson MRT on the Thomson–East Coast Line (UOL)
  • Ai Tong School within 1 km (UOL)
  • Opposite Thomson Plaza (developer’s website)
  • Panoramic views towards MacRitchie Reservoir, Windsor Nature Park and the Peirce reservoirs (UOL)
  • 2- to 5-bedroom homes only, no 1-bedroom units. The 4-Bedroom Premium types and the 5-Bedroom Suite have a private lift
  • More than 80 facilities across three clubs, including two 50 m lap pools
  • Developed by UOL, SingLand and CapitaLand Development

Design

Six towers, two heights

What the developers have published so far about the buildings, the grounds and the homes, with the artist’s impressions they’ve released.

Architecture

The architect is P&T Consultants, with Eco Plan Asia on the landscape, 2nd Edition on the interiors and Lian Beng Construction as main contractor. The four 21-storey towers make up the Classic Collection and the two 30-storey towers the Luxury Collection. Your view depends on the tower, stack and floor, so we’ll look at it unit by unit.

Grounds

About 5 hectares, roughly seven football fields, framed by Sin Ming Avenue, Upper Thomson Road and Bright Hill Drive. A chain of pools runs through the middle, and more than 80 facilities are grouped into three clubs: the Island Club, the Grand Clubhouse and the Wellness Club.

Homes

Every home has two to five bedrooms, about 592 sq ft to 1,808 sq ft. The 4-Bedroom Premium types and the 5-Bedroom Suite come with a private lift. The project kit lists Smeg, Miele, Liebherr, GROHE and BLANCO among the fittings brands, and the showflat has four show units, each styled by a different interior designer. I’ll check sizes and fittings for your unit type against the official floor plans and specifications.

Neighbourhood

UOL says the towers are placed for panoramic views towards MacRitchie Reservoir, Windsor Nature Park and the Upper and Lower Peirce reservoirs. Thomson Plaza is across the road and Upper Thomson MRT is a short walk away.

Mostly 2-bedroom

There are no 1-bedroom units. 716 of the 1,268 homes are 2-bedroom types, and 416 of those share one layout: the 678 sq ft 2-Bedroom Premium.

Homes with a private lift

The 4-Bedroom Premium, 4-Bedroom Premium + Study and 5-Bedroom Suite have a private lift: 112 homes in all. Together with the plain 4-Bedroom, the 4- and 5-bedroom homes make up 202 units, about 16% of the project.

Two collections

The Classic Collection is the four 21-storey towers, with most of the homes. The Luxury Collection is the two 30-storey towers, placed for reservoir and skyline views. Reports put them at 740 and 528 homes.

FAQs

Your questions, answered

Who is developing Thomson Reserve?

Tamarind Development Pte. Ltd. (Housing Developer’s Licence C1555), a joint venture of UOL Group, Singapore Land Group and CapitaLand Development. The architect is P&T Consultants Pte Ltd, the landscape architect Eco Plan Asia, the interior designer 2nd Edition and the main contractor Lian Beng Construction.

Where is it, and how close is the MRT?

At 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 and 11 Bright Hill Drive in District 20, opposite Thomson Plaza. UOL describes it as about a 2-minute walk from Upper Thomson MRT station on the Thomson–East Coast Line.

How many units are there, and what types?

1,268 homes in six towers (four of 21 storeys, two of 30 storeys) on a site of about 5 hectares, from 2 to 5 bedrooms. Eleven layouts are reported, from about 592 to 1,808 sq ft. Please check them against the developer’s official floor plans.

What facilities are there?

More than 80, grouped into three clubs. The Island Club has a 50 m Infinity Lap Pool and party rooms, the Grand Clubhouse has a second 50 m pool (the Canopy Lap Pool), function rooms, lounges, the main gym, a golf simulator and kids’ rooms, and the Wellness Club has a hydro spa pool, a private gym, Pilates and wellness studios. There are also tennis and multi-purpose courts, BBQ pavilions and a playground. See the site plan for where each one sits.

How much parking is there?

The project kit gives 1,014 car park lots, in one basement and a one-storey car park, for 1,268 homes.

Which homes have a private lift?

The 4-Bedroom Premium (about 1,367 sq ft), 4-Bedroom Premium + Study (about 1,485 sq ft) and 5-Bedroom Suite (about 1,808 sq ft) have a private lift. That’s 112 homes, by the reported unit counts. I’ll confirm this against the official floor plans.

What schools are close by?

Ai Tong School is within 1 km. The developer also lists Catholic High School, CHIJ St. Nicholas Girls’ School, Marymount Convent School, Raffles Institution, Raffles Girls’ School (Secondary) and Eunoia Junior College close by. For P1, check your unit’s exact distance on OneMap and the current rules on the MOE website.

What is the tenure?

99-year leasehold, starting 14 July 2026, according to the developer’s notice.

When is the preview and launch?

The public preview has been running since 17 October 2026, and the official launch is on 31 October 2026. Register or WhatsApp me and I’ll arrange your showflat visit before launch day.

When is TOP, and when can I move in?

The developer expects TOP in 2H2030, with vacant possession expected by 28 February 2031.

How much are the units?

The developer’s starting prices are S$1.544 million for a 592 sq ft two-bedroom (S$2,608 psf), S$2.503 million for a 947 sq ft three-bedroom and S$3.388 million for a 1,238 sq ft four-bedroom. These are indicative “from” prices for the cheapest unit of each type. Register and I’ll send you the full price list.

Start with your budget, CPF usage and the timing of selling your flat. My guides on using CPF for a condo and keeping your HDB while buying a condo cover the basics. This is general information, not financial advice.
