Location: MRT, shops and parks

  • Upper Thomson MRT (TE8, Thomson–East Coast Line): directly linked by a sheltered walkway (developer).
  • Bright Hill MRT (TE7): one stop away; planned Cross Island Line interchange; LTA expects CRL Phase 1, ending at Bright Hill, to be completed by 2030 (LTA Cross Island Line page).
  • Caldecott (Circle Line interchange): one stop the other way. Same-line rides to Stevens, Orchard, Outram Park, Shenton Way and Marina Bay.
  • Roads: site bounded by Upper Thomson Road (about 500 m frontage, EdgeProp), Sin Ming Avenue and Bright Hill Drive. The North–South Corridor is under construction nearby: LTA expects the viaduct (Admiralty Road West to Lentor Avenue) to open in phases from 2029 and the tunnel section (Lentor Avenue to ECP) from 2031 (LTA, Sep 2026).
  • Everyday: Thomson Plaza opposite; Upper Thomson Road dining nearby.
  • Nature: south-east of the Central Catchment Nature Reserve; views (by stack and floor) towards MacRitchie, Upper and Lower Peirce Reservoirs and Windsor Nature Park (developer).

Eight pools in all (Island Pool, two 50 m lap pools, Relaxation Bubble Pool, Leisure Pool, Kids Pool, Hydro Spa Pool and Active Pool). Towers cover 12% of the site (developer news release).

Strengths and considerations

Strengths - Sheltered link to Upper Thomson MRT; CRL interchange one stop away. - Large, green site with 88% given to landscaping and facilities. - Ai Tong School within 1 km for families planning P1. - Experienced developer team.

Considerations - "From" psf figures are for the smallest units; most units will cost more. - 716 two-bedroom homes means a lot of similar stock at resale or rental. - Vacant possession expected 28 Feb 2031, more than four years after launch. - Strong preview turnout can create pressure. Set your budget and walk-away price before booking day.

Frequently asked questions

Should I go to the Thomson Reserve showflat directly or use an agent?

Either works. For this project, commission is paid by the developer to its appointed marketing agencies, so buyers who book through an appointed agent pay no agent fee. Prices follow the developer's price list. An agent can help you shortlist stacks, run your CPF, loan and ABSD numbers and handle booking-day paperwork. Ask whether the agent's agency is appointed for the project.

How does Thomson Reserve's psf compare with nearby projects?

The developer's "from" prices are about S$2,608 psf (2-bedroom), S$2,643 psf (3-bedroom) and S$2,737 psf (4-bedroom), for the smallest unit of each type. Compare your actual stack's price with recent URA transactions at nearby resale and new condos before deciding.

Is Thomson Reserve better for own stay or investment?

For own stay, the MRT link, greenery and nearby schools are the main draws, and the long wait to 2031 matters less. For investment, consider that 716 two-bedroom units will compete for tenants and buyers, and your capital is tied up during construction. This is general information, not financial advice.

Which MRT and schools are within 1 km of Thomson Reserve?

Upper Thomson MRT (Thomson–East Coast Line) is linked by a sheltered walkway, and Ai Tong School is within 1 km, per the developer. Check other schools' distances for your unit on OneMap; 1 km does not guarantee a P1 place.

How much cash and CPF do I need, and how does progressive payment work?

For a first bank housing loan, MAS caps the loan-to-value at 75% (55% if the tenure exceeds 30 years or runs past age 65), with at least 5% of the price in cash; the rest of the downpayment can be cash or CPF. For a project under construction you pay a booking fee of 5% to 10% of the price for the Option to Purchase (Housing Developers Rules), then further instalments as construction progresses, as set out in your Sale and Purchase Agreement.

How much ABSD will I pay as a second-property owner or a PR?

Per IRAS: Singapore citizens pay 20% ABSD on a second residential property and 30% on a third; Singapore PRs pay 5% on a first, 30% on a second and 35% on a third; foreigners pay 60%. A married couple with at least one Singapore citizen who buy jointly can have the ABSD on their second home refunded if they sell their first home in time and meet IRAS's other conditions. For an uncompleted project like Thomson Reserve, the first home must be sold within 6 months after TOP or CSC, whichever is earlier.

Can I sell my HDB flat and buy Thomson Reserve?

Yes, once your flat has met its Minimum Occupation Period. Plan the order carefully: buying before selling may mean paying ABSD upfront and getting a refund later, if you qualify. With vacant possession expected in 2031, many upgraders time their HDB sale against the progressive payments. I can map out your net cash, CPF refund and timeline. See Sell HDB, buy condo.

When is TOP, and when can I move in?

The developer expects TOP in 2H2030 and vacant possession by 28 February 2031.

How does booking work on launch day?

Sales bookings open on 31 October 2026 (developer). Register your interest through an appointed agent, bring your NRIC or passport and payment towards the booking fee (5% to 10% of the price under the Housing Developers Rules), and have your shortlisted stacks and bank in-principle approval ready.

How do I choose a stack or facing?

Views depend on the tower, stack and floor: the 30-storey Luxury Collection towers are placed for reservoir and skyline views. Also check sun direction, road and MRT noise, and how close the unit is to facilities. I compare stacks unit by unit against the official site plan.
