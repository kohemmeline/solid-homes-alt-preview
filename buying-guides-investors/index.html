Frequently asked questions

What makes a condo a good investment?
Five factors: location demand, land/building quality, pricing vs comparables, rental yield and future supply constraints. Explained in the investment guide.
New launch or resale as an investment?
Each has trade-offs — new launches have premium pricing and showflat appeal; resale can offer better entry value and immediate yield. Compare on numbers.
How does ABSD affect investors?
ABSD applies to additional properties and raises the entry cost significantly. The ABSD guide details who pays and the rates.

The takeaway

Buy on the numbers and your real timeline — don’t let any single factor (school, district or launch hype) decide for you. If you’d like a second opinion on your specific situation, book a call.
