Frequently asked questions

How do I evaluate a new launch objectively?

Use a six-step framework: location fundamentals, comparison vs resale, developer track record, price per sqft, unit efficiency and exit strategy. See the evaluation guide.

New launch or resale — which is smarter?

It depends on timeline and budget. New launches offer fresh condition and showflat appeal; resale gives immediate space and yield. The comparison guide lays it out.

What are the risks of a new launch?

Delivery delays, market shift by TOP, premium pricing and limited early income. Factor these into your decision.

The takeaway

Buy on the numbers and your real timeline — don’t let any single factor (school, district or launch hype) decide for you. If you’d like a second opinion on your specific situation, book a call.
