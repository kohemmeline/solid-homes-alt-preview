04 · Independent Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

This is the first new launch in Tanjong Rhu in over 13 years — a truly boutique FREEHOLD project of just 107 units, sitting a short walk from Katong Park MRT (TEL) in a peaceful, mostly-private residential enclave. Freehold rarity plus genuine scarcity of supply is a strong combination.

Main Consideration

Be honest — pricing is at a significant premium. Sources estimate roughly 40%+ above The Line @ Tanjong Rhu nearby, and you’re paying launch-level money for a freehold in a quiet, slower precinct. Choice is thin too — as at August 2026 the balance is very limited (single digits across most layouts), so you’re picking from what’s left.

Who This Suits

Buyers who value a rare freehold in a calm, green, upmarket enclave, want the TEL for quick access to Marina Bay and town, and are comfortable paying a premium for scarcity.

Who Should Skip

The price-sensitive buyer, or anyone who wants a full range of units and layouts to choose from this late in the launch.

Own-Stay Outlook

Lovely if you love peace and green — park connectors, the waterfront feel and a boutique community, with the MRT connecting you quickly to town. A genuinely pleasant place to live if the premium doesn’t bother you.

Investment Angle

Freehold + 13-year supply drought in D15’s Tanjong Rhu is a compelling scarcity story, but the 40%+ premium over comparables means your entry point really matters. Watch the rental pool and the eventual equilibrium.

05 · Questions

FAQs

Is Arina East Residences freehold?

Yes, this is a freehold project — a genuine rarity among new launches.

Who’s the developer?

ZACD LV Development Pte Ltd, led by Hong Kong-listed ZACD Group (the site was the former La Ville, en-bloc’d for S$152m in 2021).

How many units are there?

Just 107 units across two 20-storey blocks — a boutique project.

When is TOP?

Expected around Q4 2028.

Which district is it in?

D15, on Tanjong Rhu Road.

What’s the availability and pricing?

Very limited balance as at August 2026 — roughly 2BR from ~$1.83m, 3BR around $3.2m, 4BR from ~$4m. Contact me for current inventory.

How far to the MRT?

Katong Park MRT (Thomson-East Coast Line) is roughly a 3-minute, short stroll away.

Are the schools good nearby?

Yes — Kong Hwa, Tanjong Katong Primary, Dunman High and Tanjong Katong Girls’ are all close by.

Can you register interest?

Absolutely — drop me a message and I’ll set up a viewing of the showflat. —
