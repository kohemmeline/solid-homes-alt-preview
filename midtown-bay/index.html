    Analysis

    Emmeline’s assessment

    Strongest Feature

    A 32-storey, move-in-ready luxury tower as the residential heart of the Guoco Midtown integrated project, sitting virtually on top of Bugis — with direct access to the Bugis/City Hall/Promenade/Esplanade MRT cluster. Achieved TOP in May 2024, so it’s a finished, established address.

    Main Consideration

    It’s 99-yr (from 2018) with some lease years already passed, and completed stock trades at Downtown Core prices — 1-bedders from ~$1.49M and 2-bedders ~$2.47M. The duplex options carry the remaining stock (as at August 2026), so choice for flat layouts is thinning.

    Who This Suits

    CBD professionals and buyers who want a slick, connected, integrated urban address that’s ready to move into now — walk to work, shop, dine and rail under one roof.

    Who Should Skip

    Anyone wanting a leafy family estate or bargain pricing — this is premium, compact Downtown Core living, not a big suburban condo.

    Own-Stay Outlook

    A standout for the urban dweller — Bugis hustle, dining, offices and four MRT stations on your doorstep, with the Guoco Midtown amenities below. Just embrace that it’s city-density living, not a suburban resort.

    Investment Angle

    Its position above a major interchange and inside an integrated Guoco development gives it excellent rental liquidity and CBD tenant appeal. The 99-yr lease (from 2018) and fully-built resale pricing are the factors to weigh.

04 · Questions

FAQs

Is Midtown Bay freehold?

No, it’s a 99-year leasehold, from 2018.

Who is the developer?

GuocoLand (Singapore) Pte Ltd / Guoco Midtown Pte Ltd.

How many units?

219 residential units plus 3 commercial lots.

When was TOP?

Achieved in May 2024 — it’s ready to move in.

What district is it in?

District 7, Downtown Core, at 122 Beach Road near Bugis.

Pricing and availability?

1-Bed from about $1.49M and 2-Bed ~$2.47M; duplexes carry most remaining stock (as at August 2026).

How far is the MRT?

Superb — directly connected to the Bugis area and walkable to Esplanade (~5 min), plus City Hall/Promenade close by.

Nearby amenities and schools?

Bugis Junction, Suntec and Downtown Core dining everywhere, with city schools nearby.
