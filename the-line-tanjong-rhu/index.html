04 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

This is a finished, freehold, move-in-ready project in D15 — no TOP wait, no progressive-payment uncertainty. You can see the actual product, the facilities and the view today, in a quiet, upmarket enclave that rarely trades.

Main Consideration

Be honest — you’re buying resale here, so the price depends on the individual seller, and freehold Tanjong Rhu doesn’t come cheap. It’s also a small project (130 units), so facilities are on the modest side, and as at August 2026 the available units are very limited (thin pickings across 1BR, 2BR and penthouse).

Who This Suits

Buyers who value certainty and a finished product — you get to inspect the exact unit, walk the site and see the views before committing, in a rare freehold D15 setting.

Who Should Skip

Anyone who wants a new launch (fresh lease / deferred payments / showflat energy) rather than a completed resale unit.

Own-Stay Outlook

Solid and hassle-free — an established, well-kept freehold home in a leafy, quiet enclave. What you see is what you get, which is a real comfort.

Investment Angle

Freehold and completed means you’re buying at a settled price rather than a launch premium, and Tanjong Rhu’s scarcity supports value over time. Just remember resale pricing depends on the seller and the unit you pick.
