Thomson Reserve Analysis

A strategic review of Thomson Reserve by UOL Group and CapitaLand Development, on the former Thomson View site at Bright Hill Drive. Guide first. Sell second. Real analysis, not a sales pitch.

01

Where things stand

As of 21 October 2026, Thomson Reserve is in public preview, which began on 17 October 2026. Sales bookings open on 31 October 2026. No units have been sold yet, so there are no sales or URA new-sale figures for this project at this date. I will add launch results once they are published.

The first preview weekend drew 11,800 visitors, according to the developers (reported 18 Oct 2026). Visitor numbers are not sales; treat them as a sign of interest only.

The sales gallery is at Upper Serangoon Crescent, opposite Riversails, open daily 10am to 7pm, and is not at the site itself.

05

Location and connectivity

  • Upper Thomson MRT (Thomson–East Coast Line) is linked to the development by a sheltered walkway.
  • Bright Hill MRT is one stop away and is planned as an interchange with the Cross Island Line.
  • The North–South Corridor is due to open progressively from 2029.
  • Thomson Plaza is opposite the development; Windsor Nature Park and the Upper Thomson dining stretch are within about a five-minute walk.
  • The site lies south-east of the Central Catchment Nature Reserve; the developers say more than 45% of unit stacks face it.

06

Schools

Ai Tong School is within 1km of the site. Raffles Institution, CHIJ St. Nicholas Girls' School, Catholic High School, Marymount Convent School and Eunoia Junior College are nearby, but I have not verified their exact distances; check the 1km and 2km bands for your own address with the MOE and SLA tools before relying on them for P1 registration.

07

Developer track record

UOL and CapitaLand Development's two most recent joint launches sold well: Parktown Residence in Tampines (1,193 units, launched February 2025) is 98% sold, and Skye at Holland (666 units, launched October 2025) is fully sold, as reported on 15 October 2026. Past sales do not guarantee how this launch, or its resale market, will perform.

08

Land cost in context

Thomson Reserve's land cost was about S$1,178 psf ppr (2024 deal). For comparison, recent government land sale sites went for S$1,376 and S$1,331 psf ppr at Chuan Grove (2025) and S$1,330 psf ppr at Bedok Rise (November 2025). A lower land rate does not automatically mean lower selling prices: launch prices also reflect construction costs, timing and what the developers expect the market to pay. Judge the project on its actual price list against nearby resale and new-launch transactions.

09

What you should know before buying

Strengths

  • Direct sheltered link to Upper Thomson MRT, with a Cross Island Line interchange one stop away at Bright Hill.
  • Large, green site: the towers take up 12% of the land, with 88% for landscaping and facilities (more than 80 facilities, including eight pools).
  • Ai Tong School within 1km, which matters to families planning P1 registration.
  • An experienced developer team whose two most recent joint launches sold well.

Considerations

  • "From" prices of S$2,608 to S$2,737 psf are for the smallest units; most units will cost more.
  • 1,268 units, 716 of them two-bedroom types, means a lot of similar stock when owners resell or rent.
  • Expected vacant possession is 28 February 2031, more than four years after launch, so your money is tied up during construction.
  • Strong preview turnout can create pressure to decide quickly. Set your budget and walk-away price before booking day.

FAQs

When can I book a unit?

Sales bookings open on 31 October 2026. The public preview began on 17 October 2026.

How much does Thomson Reserve cost?

Indicative starting prices are S$1.544M (592 sq ft 2-bedroom, about S$2,608 psf), S$2.503M (947 sq ft 3-bedroom) and S$3.388M (1,238 sq ft 4-bedroom). The 5-bedroom suite price has not been released.

Who is the developer?

Tamarind Development Pte. Ltd., a joint venture of CapitaLand Development, UOL Group and Singapore Land Group (a UOL subsidiary).

When will it be completed?

TOP is expected in the second half of 2030, with vacant possession expected by 28 February 2031.

Which MRT station serves it?

Upper Thomson MRT on the Thomson–East Coast Line, linked by a sheltered walkway. Bright Hill MRT, one stop away, will be an interchange with the Cross Island Line.

How many units have been sold?

None yet as of 21 October 2026: bookings open on 31 October 2026. I will update this page once launch results are published.
