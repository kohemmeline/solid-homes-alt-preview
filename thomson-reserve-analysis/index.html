01 · Project Overview

A Storied Site, Reimagined

Welcome to Thomson Reserve (汤申保留地), the landmark redevelopment of the former Thomson View Condominium — a beloved Upper Thomson icon reborn as a magnificent mega development close to MacRitchie Reservoir and the Central Catchment nature parks.

Jointly developed by UOL Group, CapitaLand Development and Singapore Land Group, Thomson Reserve brings together three of Singapore’s most esteemed developers. From 2-Bedroom to 5-Bedroom homes, set among extensive internal greenery.

06 · FAQs

Everything You Need to Know About Thomson Reserve

What is Thomson Reserve?

+

Thomson Reserve (汤申保留地) is the landmark redevelopment of the former Thomson View Condominium at Bright Hill Drive, District 20. The collective sale of Thomson View for S$810 million was agreed in October 2024 and completed in October 2025.

Who are the developers?

+

Jointly developed by UOL Group, CapitaLand Development, and Singapore Land Group (SingLand) — three of Singapore’s most reputable property developers. UOL, SingLand and CapitaLand Development have partnered before on PARKTOWN Residence and Skye at Holland.

When is the preview and launch?

+

The showflat opens on 17 October 2026. Register with Emmeline for preview access and launch updates.

How many units and what types?

+

1,268 residential units in six towers (four 21-storey and two 30-storey), from 2-Bedroom to 5-Bedroom Suite with private lift, reported at about 592 to 1,808 sq ft. The site is over 50,000 sq m.

Which MRT stations serve Thomson Reserve?

+

Upper Thomson MRT is about a 2-minute walk away (per UOL). Bright Hill and Mayflower are one and two stops away on the Thomson–East Coast Line (TEL).

What schools are nearby?

+

Ai Tong School within 1km, with Catholic High School and CHIJ St Nicholas Girls’ School within 1–2km. Raffles Institution and Eunoia Junior College are also nearby.

What is the land cost advantage?

+

Thomson Reserve was acquired at $1,178 psf/ppr — an RCR site priced below OCR land costs. For comparison, OCR sites like Chuan Grove ($1,331–$1,376 psf/ppr) and Bedok Rise ($1,330 psf/ppr) were acquired at higher land rates, making Thomson Reserve an exceptionally good value proposition considering its location.

What is the land size and tenure?

+

99-year leasehold commencing 14 July 2026. The site is over 50,000 sq m.
