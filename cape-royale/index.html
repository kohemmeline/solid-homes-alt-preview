05 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

The tallest residential tower at Sentosa Cove with 302 homes across eight blocks — marina-facing amenities and large-format 3- and 4-bedroom layouts, offering a real choice of waterfront living at a more approachable psf than many of its ultra-exclusive Cove neighbours.

Main Consideration

Even being the “more abundant” option at the Cove, there’s still no MRT — the nearest is HarbourFront across the causeway, so this is a car/shuttle lifestyle, not a train one. And at ~$2,148–$2,417 psf on Sentosa, it’s still a serious commitment.

Who This Suits

Buyers who want genuine marina/waterfront living with more layout choice than the tiny Sentosa boutiques, and who accept the car-dependent island lifestyle.

Who Should Skip

Anyone who needs MRT access or expects the lower psf to mean “affordable” — it’s cheaper than its peers but still a premium waterfront price.

Own-Stay Outlook

A spacious, well-run resort-style home on the water with real community breadth — ideal if sea views and the Sentosa lifestyle are what you’re after.

Investment Angle

With 302 units it has healthier trading volume than the tiny Cove projects, and Sentosa’s waterfront scarcity underpins value — but the niche buyer pool and island access remain factors to weigh.

06 · Questions

FAQs

Is Cape Royale freehold?

No, it’s a 99-year leasehold.

Who is the developer?

Pinnacle (Sentosa) Pte Ltd.

How many units are there?

302 units across eight residential blocks.

When was it completed (TOP)?

2013, so a completed resale.

What district is it in?

District 4, at 11/25 Cove Way, Sentosa Cove.

What’s available and pricing?

3BR from ~$3.5M, 4BR from ~$5.3M, and a few penthouses from ~$6.2M, with good availability for the Cove (as at August 2026).

Which MRT is nearest?

HarbourFront MRT, not walkable from Sentosa — you’d drive or take the shuttle.

What’s nearby?

Marina, Quayside Isle dining, beaches and the full Sentosa resort precinct.
