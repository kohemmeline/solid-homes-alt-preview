About Town Hall Link

Strategically located within the Jurong Lake District, Singapore’s second CBD, Town Hall Link offers residents a front-row seat to one of the nation’s most ambitious urban development plans. With direct access to Jurong East’s comprehensive transport hub, world-class shopping, and the lush Jurong Lake Gardens, this OCR development represents an exceptional living opportunity.

As a URA GLS 2H 2026 Confirmed List site, this development represents one of the most anticipated residential launches in Singapore. Register your interest with Emmeline Koh for priority queue numbers and exclusive preview access. Official 2H2026 GLS details: this white site on Town Hall Link is part of the Government’s commitment to developing the Jurong Lake District as Singapore’s largest mixed-use business district outside the city centre – an estimated up to about 1,200 residential units plus substantial office (min 40,000 sqm) and commercial space (83,200 sqm). It sits adjacent to the Jurong East transport hub with connectivity to multiple MRT lines (East-West, North-South, Jurong Region and Cross Island), and will incorporate a District Cooling Plant and District Pneumatic Waste Conveyance System Central Station.
