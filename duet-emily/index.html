05 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

A rare freehold in a charming, leafy little enclave up on Mount Emily — just 20 exclusive units, so it’s intimate and quiet, and the freehold-in-D9 story is genuinely scarce. For a freehold close to the city, the entry from ~$1.01M is comparatively approachable.

Main Consideration

It’s a tiny (20-unit) boutique project — no big clubhouse or resort-style land, so the amenities and facilities are modest. Little India/Rochor are close by, and the streets up on Mount Emily are hilly, so the MRT walk isn’t a flat stroll. Availability is razor-thin across all layouts (as at August 2026).

Who This Suits

Buyers who value the scarcity of D9 freehold, want something small and exclusive, and don’t need a full-amenity condo experience — perfect for a lock-up city pied-à-terre too.

Who Should Skip

Families wanting clubhouse facilities and big grounds, or anyone who needs a fast, flat walk to the MRT — the boutique size and hilltop location won’t suit everyone.

Own-Stay Outlook

Lovely for the right buyer — peace, privacy and freehold permanence in a character-rich city enclave. Just be honest that it’s boutique and compact, with a hilly setting rather than resort-style living.

Investment Angle

Freehold in D9 is a strong store-of-value story and 20-unit scarity adds to the appeal; the low unit count means limited rental stock, which can support pricing. The exclusive niche profile is the offset — demand is narrow.
