HDB Upgraders

Best New Launches for HDB Upgraders Singapore 2026

As of July 2026, HDB upgraders face a market where ABSD, TDSR limits and elevated new launch pricing make every decision critical. Here is Emmeline’s assessment of which new launches are worth your attention — and which ones to approach carefully.

By Emmeline Koh · Updated July 2026

01

Top Picks for HDB Upgraders

Lucerne Grand

Why for upgraders: Lakeside MRT doorstep means car-light living is realistic. Entry pricing from ~$1.2M makes it one of the more accessible new launches. Jurong Lake District transformation provides medium-term upside. The 575-unit development has good rental prospects if you decide to hold.

Trade-off: OCR location further from city centre than some alternatives.

Pinetree Hill

Why for upgraders: UOL + SingLand quality gives confidence. 3BR options from ~$2.1M work for upgraders with HDB sale proceeds. School belt location (Dover Court, ACS International) suits families. Strong capital preservation potential given developer pedigree.

Trade-off: MRT at ~1.2km — factor in shuttle or car costs.

Sora (Yuan Ching Road)

Why for upgraders: Competitive psf pricing for Jurong area. Budget-friendly entry point for west-side upgraders who want to stay in the region. Jurong Lake District upside potential.

Trade-off: Surrounding environment still developing — patience required for full transformation.

Dunearn House

Why for upgraders: Turf City masterplan positions this as a long-term play. Bukit Timah address carries cachet. Good family layouts. The broader masterplan means improving amenities over time.

Trade-off: Masterplan still early-stage — near-term amenities may be limited.
