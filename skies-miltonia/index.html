05 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

A peaceful, resort-style condo nestled by the water in the greenery of north Yishun — the kind of calm, scenic setting quiet-lovers appreciate, with nicely sized penthouse and townhouse homes rather than the usual cookie-cutter 2-bedder stack.

Main Consideration

Honest one: it’s not near an MRT. Khatib is about 1.5km and Yishun a touch further — it’s a shuttle-bus, bus or drive situation, which the low-density serenity buys you. It’s also a 2016 build now, so as a resale the big-ticket remaining options (penthouses around $2.6m, townhouses ~$3.45m) are significant outlays in an out-of-the-way location.

Who This Suits

Families and upgraders who prize peace, greenery and space-per-dollar, and who’re fine with a car- or bus-dependent lifestyle in the north.

Who Should Skip

Commuters who need an MRT within walking distance, and buyers looking for a compact, low-cost entry in Yishun.

Own-Stay Outlook

Very comfortable if you love a tranquil, cinematic, water-adjacent home and you’re not rail-dependent daily — a genuinely lovely place to live for the right household.

Investment Angle

The Lincoln/waterfront feel and low supply near the future North-South Corridor give it a decent hold story, but the MRT distance and age mean the rental drivers are softer than central launches — more home than pure investment play.

06 · Questions

FAQs

Is Skies Miltonia freehold?

No, it’s 99-year leasehold, commencing 2012.

Who’s the developer?

TG Master Pte Ltd of the TG Group.

How many units?

420 units.

When was it completed?

TOP in 2016, so it’s a completed, matured residence.

What district is it in?

D27 (Yishun / Sembawang).

What’s the pricing and availability?

Resale penthouses from ~$2.6m and townhouses around $3.45m; a handful of these premium units remain listed as at August 2026.

How far is the MRT?

Not a short walk — Khatib MRT is ~1.5km and Yishun slightly further; there’s a shuttle/bus to both.

What’s nearby?

Wisteria Mall, Northpoint City, Northbrooks Secondary and international schools close by.
