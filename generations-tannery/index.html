Project Overview

01

Singapore’s Rarest Asset Class: Freehold B1 Industrial

Most new industrial land in Singapore today is sold on 20–33 year leasehold terms (IGLS). Freehold B1 industrial in a central location has become extraordinarily rare. Generations @ Tannery delivers exactly that — a hard-to-find combination of perpetual land value, modern specifications, and city-fringe accessibility.

Located in the Kallang-Mattar precinct, the development is an 510m / 6-minute walk from Mattar MRT (via Mattar Road), with direct access to PIE and Aljunied Road. The surrounding area is undergoing a live-work-play transformation with new BTO projects, mixed-use developments, and the upcoming Gourmet Xchange food hub.

The Investment Case

03

Why Freehold B1 Industrial?

Three structural tailwinds make this a compelling long-term play.

Scarcity & Demand

Freehold B1 in central locations is extremely limited. Most new supply is 20–33 year leasehold pushed to the outskirts. Multi-user factory prices have risen 33.5%, with 22 straight quarters of rental growth. Occupancy at 90.2%.

Tax Efficiency

No ABSD or SSD — unlike residential where foreigners pay up to 60% ABSD and companies pay 65%. Up to 90% LTV for owner-occupiers. GST is payable but fully claimable upon TOP.

Legacy Asset

Freehold land holds perpetual value. Unlike leasehold where the asset decays over time, freehold industrial provides capital preservation across generations. The “unfair advantage” — new specs + perpetual land.
