05 · Questions

FAQs

Is Emerald of Katong freehold?

No, it’s 99-year leasehold, in District 15 Katong / Meyer / East Coast.

Who’s the developer?

Sim Lian — a developer with a long track record in Singapore residential launches, which matters for build quality and after-sales.

How many units?

857 units at Upper East Coast, making it a substantial single development in prime D15.

What district is it in?

D15 Katong / Meyer / East Coast — one of Singapore’s most established and desirable residential enclaves, with strong heritage value and upcoming transformation.

What’s the main appeal of the location?

The established Katong lifestyle — heritage, dining and neighbourhood character — with East Coast Park and green space close by. It suits buyers who want a recognised east-side address over a short-term flip.

Who is Emerald of Katong best suited to?

Longer-term, patient buyers — especially families who value the Katong enclave and the developer’s track record — rather than those chasing a quick gain. Own-stay outlook here is excellent.

What should I consider before buying?

Weigh the entry pricing against other D15 new launches and resale options, and check the lease-remainder against your holding period and how banks will treat it on resale. This rewards holding long-term.

What comparable projects should I look at?

Nearby in D15: Claydence (FREEHOLD boutique, 28 units, Still Road, 1BR from about S$1.658M, TOP 2026) and The Line @ Tanjong Rhu (FREEHOLD, 130 units, waterfront, limited 1BR from about S$1.915M).

Can I use CPF or bank financing?

Yes — CPF OA and bank loans both apply to private condos in Singapore, subject to the usual ABSD, LTV and lease-remainder rules. Confirm your financing approval before committing.
