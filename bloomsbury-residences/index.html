04 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

This is a premium 99-year leasehold project right in the one-north innovation hub, a short walk from one-north and Buona Vista MRT — an area with strong tech/bio-tech employment and continuing growth. It carries a small retail component too, adding everyday convenience.

Main Consideration

Be honest — one-north is still an area that’s more about work than abundant lifestyle, and the premium pricing reflects the brand and the location. As at August 2026 many units have sold (2BR with single digits left) though the 4BR and penthouse suites still have a wider range (22 x 4BR Suite+Flexi, smaller numbers on penthouses).

Who This Suits

Professionals, especially in tech, research and media, who want to live close to one-north/ Buona Vista, value the strong employment hub and don’t mind the premium.

Who Should Skip

Anyone who wants a lively, built-out lifestyle neighbourhood right now, or a bigger project with more extensive facilities.

Own-Stay Outlook

Very good for tech and research workers who want a short commute and a smart, modern home in the heart of one-north. Just be prepared for a work-centric, still-building precinct around you.

Investment Angle

One-north’s sustained employment growth and consistent rental demand from the tech sector give this real legs, but you’re paying a premium entry. The retail component and the growing precinct should support values as the area matures.

05 · Questions

FAQs

Is Bloomsbury Residences freehold?

No, it’s a 99-year leasehold.

Who’s the developer?

Media Circle Development Pte Ltd (the one-north / Media Circle consortium).

How many units are there?

358 units, from 2-bedroom up to penthouses, across three blocks.

When is TOP?

Expected around November 2028.

Which district is it in?

D5, at Media Circle in the one-north precinct.

What’s the availability and pricing?

As at August 2026, roughly 2BR Premium from ~$1.76m, 4BR from ~$3.6m, penthouses up to ~$4.7m. Contact me for current inventory.

How far to the MRT?

Short walk to one-north MRT (Circle Line), with Buona Vista interchange (a major Circle/ East-West hub) also close by.

Is it a mixed development?

Yes, it includes around 400 sqm of retail on the first storey for everyday convenience.
