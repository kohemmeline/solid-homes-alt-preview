04 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

A completed 8-storey freehold in Geylang at a rare city-fringe price point — you can get into a freehold near the city for a starting price around $1M, which is genuinely uncommon. Aljunied MRT is close, so connectivity is real.

Main Consideration

It’s a tiny 24-unit development, and it’s in the Geylang area — a lively, somewhat mixed neighbourhood that isn’t the quietest. Very few units left (as at August 2026) and amenities are more local-scale than polished.

Who This Suits

First-timers and value buyers who want freehold tenure near the city at an accessible entry price and don’t mind a busy, urban setting.

Who Should Skip

Anyone who wants a tranquil, resort-style development, or who prefers the polished feel of a large condo.

Own-Stay Outlook

A sensible, affordable freehold foothold close to the city — fine if you’re happy with Geylang’s energy and the boutique scale.

Investment Angle

Freehold at that price point near Aljunied/City is the draw; rental demand in Geylang is steady, but the compact size and neighbourhood character mean growth is more about location than scale.
