About Tanjong Rhu Close

Set in the tranquil Tanjong Rhu enclave along the scenic Kallang Basin, this RCR development offers a serene waterfront lifestyle with unparalleled city views. Residents enjoy the best of both worlds — peaceful riverside living with swift access to the Marina Bay CBD and Geylang’s renowned dining scene.

As a URA GLS 2H 2026 Confirmed List site, this development represents one of the most anticipated residential launches in Singapore. Register your interest with Emmeline Koh for priority queue numbers and exclusive preview access. Official 2H2026 GLS details: this RCR site on Tanjong Rhu Close has a plot ratio of 3.5 with an estimated yield of about 505 residential units. It is about a 10-minute walk from Katong Park MRT (Thomson-East Coast Line), near Dunman High, Chung Cheng High and Broadrick Secondary, and close to the National Stadium, Kallang River basin and East Coast Park with potential scenic views across Marina East and the sea. The most recent GLS site at the adjacent Tanjong Rhu Road plot was awarded in February 2026.
