Buying a Condo in District 4: Sentosa / Southern Islands

Resort living on Sentosa and the Southern Islands — waterfront lifestyle. Here’s the honest picture for buyers — both new launches and resale.

Updated 2026-10-22

Quick Answer

District 4 suits luxury buyers and investors seeking unique waterfront properties.. It’s a strong, defensible choice if you buy on location, school catchment and genuine lifestyle — be disciplined on price against the alternatives and don’t chase a hope of quick upside.

What I’d do

If you’re a family settling or an upgrader, District 4 is a legitimate choice — buy on location and school catchment rather than a hope of quick appreciation. If you’re investing, compare hard against what’s available one district over, and judge each project on its own numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use CPF to buy a condo in Sentosa?

Yes — CPF OA can be used for private condos (subject to the applicable limits and lease-remainder rule). See the full CPF guide for the mechanics.

Is Sentosa good as an investment right now?

It depends on your horizon and the specific project. Compare new launches against resale and the surrounding supply before committing — I cover this in the project pages.

New launch or resale in Sentosa?

New launches in Sentosa vary in availability and pricing; resale can offer better space-per-dollar. Decide on your timeline and how much renovation you want to take on.

The takeaway

District 4 rewards you with lifestyle, schools and a genuine neighbourhood — pay for those, not for a hope of quick upside.
