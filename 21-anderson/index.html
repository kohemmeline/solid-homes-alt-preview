05 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

Ultra-exclusive ~18-unit freehold in prime D10, high-spec A&A restoration, strictly boutique scale with central Tanglin location.

Main Consideration

Extreme pricing (2BR at S$10M, 4BR at S$22.5M) and strict by-appointment access — for UHNW buyers only.

Who This Suits

Ultra-high-net-worth buyers seeking an intimate freehold luxury estate in a prime central D10 location.

Who Should Skip

Mainstream buyers and first-timers — boutique scale and premium pricing exclude broad market.

Own-Stay OutlookExceptional for prestige own-stay — private, freehold, central and intimately scaled.

Investment Angle

Exclusive D10 freehold scarcity supports value, but entry at S$10M+ suits few investors. resale positioning will be defined by prevailing market conditions.
