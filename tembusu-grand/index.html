04 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

It’s the quality-story — CDL/MCL built, recently completed (2025), so you can actually walk into a finished, take-key-now home in the sought-after Tanjong Katong/Dakota belt — no construction wait. The Katong food-and-lifestyle heritage around it is a huge draw.

Main Consideration

Because it’s effectively sold out into resale, your choice is the tail end — a couple of 1-bedders and the penthouses (as at August 2026). You’re also buying into the premium Tanjong Katong resale market on a 99-yr lease that started in 2022, so the price already reflects the district and the completed pedigree.

Who This Suits

Buyers who want a move-in-ready, developer-branded home in the evergreen Katong/Dakota neighbourhood, without the multi-year construction wait — and who don’t need a full range of layouts.

Who Should Skip

Anyone wanting fresh block-selling prices or a full choice of unit types — at this stage it’s limited resale stock, and the penthouses are a very different price league.

Own-Stay Outlook

Delightful — finished quality from CDL/MCL, right in the vibrant Tanjong Katong dining enclave, with Amber/Dakota MRT access and the sea-side lifestyle minutes away. If you’re happy with the residual stock, it’s a lovely move-in.

Investment Angle

Tanjong Katong and Marine Parade are a proven, liquid resale and rental belt with strong family demand, and a completed CDL brand trades well. The lease started in 2022 and remaining stock is thin, so pickings are the consideration more than the area.

05 · Questions

FAQs

Is Tembusu Grand freehold?

No, it’s a 99-year leasehold, from 25 April 2022.

Who is the developer?

CDL Triton Pte. Ltd., a JV between City Developments Ltd (CDL) and MCL Land.

How many units?

638 units across 4 towers.

When was TOP?

Completed in 2025 (now resale/limited stock).

What district is it in?

District 15, at 92–98 Jalan Tembusu, near Tanjong Katong/Dakota.

Pricing and availability?

Limited resale: 1-Bed + Study from about $1.41M and penthouses around $7.89M (as at August 2026).

How far is the MRT?

Close to Amber MRT (TE25) and within reach of Dakota; the Katong area is well-served.

Nearby amenities and schools?

Tanjong Katong’s famous food street, Parkway Parade and East Coast vibes minutes away, with top schools in the district.

Can I register interest?

For resale/last units yes — Em can track the remaining stock and arrange a viewing.
