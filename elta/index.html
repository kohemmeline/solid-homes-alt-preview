04 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

It’s in established Clementi — one of the West’s most liveable towns, with the MRT and bus interchange right there, a proper town centre, a strong school belt and NUS nearby. You’re buying into a complete, proven neighbourhood, not a promise.

Main Consideration

It’s been selling well, so balance stock is limited in several layouts (as at August 2026), and the price has crept up as the launch has gone through. You’ll need to act on what’s actually left.

Who This Suits

West-side upgraders, NUS families and students, and investors who know Clementi’s rental depth — this is a location that rents and resells well.

Who Should Skip

Buyers who want a wide choice of units or are strictly capped at a budget that no longer fits the remaining stock.

Own-Stay Outlook

Very solid. Few towns in the West match Clementi for everyday convenience — walk to the interchange, the shops, the food, and have decent schools within reach.

Investment Angle

Clementi’s demand runs deep — NUS, the tech park and the medical cluster keep rental interest steady, and the interchange keeps it connected. That combination supports both rental and resale.

05 · Questions

FAQs

Is ELTA freehold?

No, it’s a 99-year leasehold development.

Who is the developer?

MCL Land and CSC Land, via the HC Land (Clementi) joint venture.

How many units are there?

501 units across two 39-storey towers.

When is TOP?

Expected Q1 2028.

What district is it in?

District 5, Clementi.

What’s available and pricing?

1-bedroom + study through to 4-bedroom dual-key and premium layouts, from about $1.40M (as at August 2026).

How close is the MRT?

Near Clementi MRT and bus interchange on the East–West Line.

What’s nearby?

Clementi town centre, NUS, and the Clementi school belt including Anglo-Chinese and Nan Hua.
