05 · Independent Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

A rare freehold launch in the Boon Keng / D12 area — just about a freehold haven next to few freehold towers. Boon Keng MRT is a real 5–7 minute walk on the North-East Line, so you get that honest MRT access with freehold tenure, in a 172-unit 24-storey tower.

Main Consideration

It’s a new build, so you’re waiting until roughly end-2028 (TOP details to confirm [verify]) — that’s a few years out. Around Boon Keng the local retail is more heartland market/food-centre scale than big malls, which suits some and not others.

Who This Suits

Buyers who want freehold tenure plus genuine MRT access in a central-ish D12 location and are willing to wait for the build.

Who Should Skip

Anyone who needs a home soon, or who wants doorstep big-mall shopping rather than a heartland setting.

Own-Stay Outlook

Well-placed for city access — three stops to Dhoby Ghaut — with a nice green, landscaped tower; a good freehold long-term home if the wait works for you.

Investment Angle

Freehold in D12 near Boon Keng MRT is the classic scarcity-plus-connectivity pattern, with solid upside as the area and its infrastructure mature.
