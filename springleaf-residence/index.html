05 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

A rare first — the first high-rise development in the landed enclave of Springleaf, right beside Springleaf MRT (TE4) on the Thomson-East Coast Line, with a conserved former Upper Thomson Secondary School block woven into the design. Top-tier developer pedigree (GuocoLand + Hong Leong) and it flew off the shelf at launch — 92% sold in a weekend.

Main Consideration

Honest word on how far along it is — it’s a big, pricey project (941 units, ~$2.1k+ psf) that’s already largely sold; at launch ~92% was taken. That means the remaining choice here is mainly the larger 3- and 5-bedder layouts in the conserved building, and the bigger-ticket price tag is real.

Who This Suits

Upgraders and families who want a brand-new high-rise in a leafy landed neighbourhood with an MRT at the doorstep and strong-schools appeal.

Who Should Skip

Anyone on a tighter budget looking for small affordable units — full-range 1-2 bedders are gone and the bulk remaining is premium 3-5 bedders.

Own-Stay Outlook

Excellent — new high-rise living, unblocked views over the landed estate, heritage character, and a two-minute MRT walk. A genuinely compelling family home if the premium fits.

Investment Angle

The scorching launch demand (92% take-up at ~$2,175 psf) plus MRT-adjacent scarcity in a landed-residential zone makes for a strong long-term story, though entry costs are high so the yield math is more about capital growth than near-term rental.

06 · Questions

FAQs

Is Springleaf Residence freehold?

No, it’s 99-year leasehold, commencing 15 July 2024.

Who’s the developer?

A joint venture of GuocoLand and Hong Leong Holdings.

How many units?

941 residential units (909 in 5 towers + 32 in the conserved block), giving 1-5 bedders.

When is TOP?

Expected 2H 2029, with vacant possession targeted by 31 Dec 2031.

What district is it in?

D26 (Upper Thomson / Springleaf).

What’s the pricing and availability?

From ~$2.732m for a 3-Bedder; remaining stock is mostly larger 3- and 5-bedder layouts in the conserved building, limited as at August 2026.

How far is the MRT?

Right beside Springleaf MRT (TE4), roughly a two-minute walk.

What’s nearby?

Springleaf Nature Park, Thomson dining strip, and a strong school belt around Upper Thomson.
