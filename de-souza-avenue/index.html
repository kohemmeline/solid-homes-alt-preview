About De Souza Avenue

Nestled in the prestigious Bukit Timah vicinity, De Souza Avenue offers a rare opportunity to live in one of Singapore’s most coveted residential enclaves. Surrounded by lush greenery, premier schools, and exclusive amenities, this RCR development represents the quintessential address for discerning families.

As a URA GLS 2H 2026 Confirmed List site, this development represents one of the most anticipated residential launches in Singapore. Register your interest with Emmeline Koh for priority queue numbers and exclusive preview access. Official 2H2026 GLS details: this RCR site on De Souza Avenue has a plot ratio of 1.6 with an estimated yield of about 415 residential units. It sits about a 15-20 minute walk from Beauty World MRT (Downtown Line) and the upcoming Beauty World Integrated Transport Hub, one station from King Albert Park MRT which is slated to be a Cross Island Line interchange. It is within 1km of Bukit Timah Primary and within 1-2km of Keming Primary, Lianhua Primary and Pei Hwa Presbyterian Primary. It follows the De Souza Avenue GLS site now developed as The Sen (awarded August 2024).
