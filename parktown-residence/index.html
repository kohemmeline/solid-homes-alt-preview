05 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

Honest answer — this is Singapore’s first true mega-integrated Tampines North hub, and that scale is hard to beat. It’s a rare big mixed-use launch sitting right in the new Tampines North precinct, so you’re getting a near-vertical township with retail, transport and community all baked into one site. The scale and the freshness of the precinct are genuinely impressive.

Main Consideration

It’s almost fully sold — only the larger 4 and 5-bedroom premium units are left, and those run into the $3.3M-to-$3.9M range (as at August 2026). So if you were dreaming of a smaller, more affordable unit here, that ship has largely sailed. Also, Tampines North as a precinct is still being built out, so you’re buying into a work-in-progress vibrant future, not a finished one.

Who This Suits

Buyers who love the idea of living in a brand-new mega-township with sizeable multi-gen family floorplans and deep pockets for the larger premium units still available.

Who Should Skip

Anyone wanting a modest-priced or compact unit, or anyone not willing to wait — TOP is still a few years out and the surrounding precinct isn’t fully mature yet.

Own-Stay Outlook

Very strong for families who want to stay put long-term — the integrated design and the fresh Tampines North amenities will make daily life genuinely convenient once the estate fills in.

Investment Angle

Tampines North is the eastern growth story and big integrated sites on this scale are scarce in D18, so there’s real medium-term appeal — but with TOP still years away and a hefty entry price on the remaining units, you need a patient, long-horizon approach rather than a flip.

06 · Questions

FAQs

Is Parktown Residence freehold?

No, it’s 99-year leasehold from October 2023; not freehold but that’s the norm for a modern mega integrated site in Tampines.

Who is the developer?

A joint venture of UOL Group, CapitaLand Development and Singapore Land (SingLand), three of the most established names in the market.

How many units does it have?

About 1,193 residential units, one of the biggest launches around.

When is the TOP?

Expected around June 2030 (as at August 2026), so still a few years to completion.

Which district is it in?

D18, in the new Tampines North precinct off Tampines Street 62 / Tampines Avenue 11.

Is it fully sold out? What’s the pricing?

Not fully, but only large 4 and 5-bedroom premium units remain from around $3.3M upward (as at August 2026).

How close is it to the MRT?

It sits within the Tampines North integrated hub connected to the new Tampines North MRT on the TEL; check the exact walk on the site plan as the block-to-lift distance varies [verify: precise walk distance to Tampines North MRT].

What’s nearby / schools?

The integrated development brings retail, dining and community amenities on-site, with school options around Tampines North; your nearest established schools and malls grow as the precinct builds out.
