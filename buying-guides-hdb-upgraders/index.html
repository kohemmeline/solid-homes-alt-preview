05

Frequently asked questions

Can I keep my HDB and still buy a condo?

Only in specific situations (e.g. conditions on MOP and family structure). The keep-hdb guide covers when it works and the real costs.

Do I need to sell my HDB before buying a condo?

Not always — timing and financing matter. The buy-before-selling guide walks through the trade-offs.

How much do I need to upgrade?

It hinges on the gap between your HDB sale + CPF/cash vs the condo price, plus ABSD. See the how-much and ABSD guides.

The takeaway

Buy on the numbers and your real timeline — don’t let any single factor (school, district or launch hype) decide for you. If you’d like a second opinion on your specific situation, book a call.
