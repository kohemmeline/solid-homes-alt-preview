Singapore Area Guides

District-by-district property insights for Singapore buyers and investors. Each guide covers pricing trends, school access, MRT connectivity, and development outlook.

Singapore is divided into 28 districts, each with its own character, price profile, and buyer demographics. Whether you are looking at prime CCR districts like D9 (Orchard) and D10 (Bukit Timah), upgrading in RCR areas like D14 (Geylang) and D15 (East Coast), or seeking value in OCR zones like D21 (Clementi Park) and D23 (Bukit Panjang), understanding the district dynamics is the first step to a sound property decision.
