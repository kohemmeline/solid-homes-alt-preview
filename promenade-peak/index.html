05 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

A 63-storey landmark on the prime Zion Road strip — the kind of height that brings genuine river and city views at the top floors, in a patch of town with real dining character. It’s a rare big new build in this corner of the Core Central Region.

Main Consideration

Fresh 99-yr lease means the premium is high for a leasehold, and the entry price sits comfortably in the $1.4M+ range for a 1-bedder. The 3-bedroom options are already very thin (single digits left as at August 2026), so if you wanted a 3-bed on the river you’ve got little to choose from.

Who This Suits

Buyers who want a premium, sky-scraping city address close to the river and CBD, and who are comfortable paying top dollar for a 99-year lease.

Who Should Skip

Anyone who needs freehold or a more modest budget for a 1-2 bedder — Promenade Peak’s pricing and lease structure are firmly premium.

Own-Stay Outlook

A delight for those who love the river-valley lifestyle — evening strolls along Zion Road, easy CBD access, and the wow factor of high floors. The Havelock/River Valley MRT walk is genuinely decent, so commuters are well served.

Investment Angle

The Zion Road corridor is a mature, sought-after rental belt with strong tenant demand, and 63-storey river-facing stock is scarce. The 99-yr lease is the main thing to weigh against the freehold alternatives in the area.

06 · Questions

FAQs

Is Promenade Peak freehold?

No, it’s a 99-year leasehold development.

Who is the developer?

Allgreen Properties Ltd.

How many units?

596 units in a single 63-storey block.

When is TOP?

Expected in Q4 2029.

What district is it in?

District 3, at 1 Zion Promenade along Zion Road.

Pricing and availability?

From about $1.46M for a 1-Bedroom + Study; 3-bedroom stock is very thin (as at August 2026).

How far is the MRT?

Roughly a 3-minute walk to River Valley MRT and about 8 minutes to Havelock MRT (TEL).

Nearby amenities and schools?

River Valley’s dining strip, Robertson Quay and the CBD are on your doorstep, with a good set of city schools nearby.
