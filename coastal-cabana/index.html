04 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

It’s the first EC launch in the Jalan Loyang area in over a decade — big, family-friendly layouts at an EC price point in the Pasir Ris–Changi east. For eligible buyers it’s a lot of space for the money.

Main Consideration

This is an Executive Condominium, so it’s not for everyone. You must meet the eligibility rules (income ceiling), and a 5-year minimum occupation period (MOP) applies — you can’t rent it out or sell it on the open market during that window. So it’s a genuine own-stay commitment, not a quick-flip play.

Who This Suits

First-timers, HDB upgraders and families who qualify for EC and want a spacious, well-priced new home in the east, happy to commit to the MOP.

Who Should Skip

Buyers who don’t meet the EC income/eligibility criteria, or who need short-term flexibility — to rent out or sell quickly. Those buyers should look at private condos instead.

Own-Stay Outlook

Solid — generous layouts, a coastal setting and a family-friendly price. As an own-stay home, the EC restrictions matter less because you’re living in it.

Investment Angle

The EC pricing plus the Changi/Pasir Ris economy give reasonable entry value, but the MOP lock means this only makes sense for those who can hold and commit.

05 · Questions

FAQs

Is Coastal Cabana freehold?

No, it’s a 99-year leasehold Executive Condominium (EC).

Who is the developer?

A joint venture of China Communications Construction (CCCC), ZACD Group and Qingjian Realty.

How many units are there?

748 units across 16 blocks.

When is TOP?

Expected around March 2029.

What district is it in?

District 17/18, Jalan Loyang Besar, Pasir Ris.

What’s available and pricing?

3-bedroom Deluxe/Premium to 4-bedroom layouts, from about $1.54M (as at August 2026).

Am I eligible?

EC buyers must meet income and eligibility rules, and the 5-year MOP applies — contact Emmeline to check your situation.

How close is the MRT?

Near Pasir Ris MRT (East–West Line), with the future Cross Island Line also planned for the area.

How do I register interest?

Contact Emmeline via WhatsApp or the CTA to check eligibility and see available units. —
