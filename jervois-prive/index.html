04 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

A completed low-rise freehold in prime D10 — 5 storeys, boutique scale, and actually ready to move into. Redhill MRT is about an 8-minute walk, so real connectivity for a freehold in this corridor is a strong card.

Main Consideration

It’s a small project (43–45 units) with very few units left (as at August 2026), so choice is limited. Freehold at these prices means a premium psf, and it’s on a 5-storey scale rather than a full resort-style development.

Who This Suits

Buyers who want a finished freehold home with genuine MRT access in D10 and are happy to pick from a small remaining pool.

Who Should Skip

Anyone wanting a big development with full facilities, or who wants a broad range of layouts to choose from.

Own-Stay Outlook

Lovely for a lock-up-and-go or small-family freehold in a quiet, established part of D10 — near Redhill and Tiong Bahru’s food scene, and ready to live in.

Investment Angle

Freehold D10 with a real MRT walk and proven rental demand is a solid value-holding play; the compact unit count keeps supply tight, which supports pricing over time.
