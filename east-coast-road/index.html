About East Coast Road

A boutique residential gem along the vibrant East Coast Road, this development offers the perfect blend of coastal living and urban convenience. Situated in District 15, residents enjoy easy access to East Coast Park’s beautiful beaches, Siglap’s trendy dining scene, and excellent schools.

As a URA GLS 2H 2026 Confirmed List site, this development represents one of the most anticipated residential launches in Singapore. Register your interest with Emmeline Koh for priority queue numbers and exclusive preview access. Official 2H2026 GLS details: this small OCR low-rise plot on East Coast Road has a plot ratio of 1.6 with an estimated yield of about 85 residential units (comparable to surrounding private landed homes), set in a largely landed housing enclave. It is about a 10-minute walk from Siglap MRT (Thomson-East Coast Line), within 1km of Ngee Ann Primary, Opera Estate Primary and St Stephen’s School, about 15 minutes’ walk from East Coast Park, and one MRT stop from the upcoming Bayshore lifestyle node. It follows the nearby Siglap Road site now developed as Seaside Residences (awarded January 2016).
