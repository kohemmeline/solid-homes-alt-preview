04 · Analysis

Emmeline’s assessment

Strongest Feature

A freehold boutique in the established East Coast/D15 enclave, tucked along Still Road with easy access to the ECP, PIE and KPE. It’s basically complete, so you can see exactly what you’re buying — and freehold near the east coast is always in demand.

Main Consideration

Honest truth — the MRT is not a doorstep walk. Eunos is about 10–12 minutes away, and there’s no big shopping right at the door, though Parkway Parade and Katong V are close. Also, it’s a 28-unit project with very few units left (as at August 2026), so your options are thin.

Who This Suits

Buyers who value freehold tenure in a mature east-coast neighbourhood and are prepared to live the car/bus lifestyle rather than stepping straight onto a train.

Who Should Skip

Anyone who needs an MRT within a short walk, or who wants a big development with full amenities and a wide choice of units.

Own-Stay Outlook

Charming and liveable for those who love the East Coast — close to the park, food and the sea breeze — provided you’re comfortable with the MRT distance and boutique scale.

Investment Angle

Freehold in D15 with a mature rental pool holds value well; the walk to the station caps the premium a bit, but long land tenure and East Coast scarcity are the upside story.
